# 翻片矩阵字标组装｜像素激活、平面组字、厚度长出与卡片收束 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one fixed cell matrix, one exact short word and a neutral unflipped baseline. Propagate local cell activation until the complete flat word is readable without misspelling. Grow registered letter depth, layers and one accent while preserving every character position. Use bounded extrusion and recoil so modules return to a stable aligned state. Compress the resolved dimensional word into one clean card and hold the exact lockup. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.
