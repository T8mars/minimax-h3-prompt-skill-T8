# 单色激活｜唯一色彩信号跨尺度接力 Template

## Mechanism

Build a world from one narrow low-saturation color family and establish at least three readable scales before activation. Introduce exactly one high-saturation accent hue at a small local source, then preserve hue identity while moving the signal through a visible carrier to a larger structure and finally to an environment-wide network. Each scale transition must retain prior monochrome anchors and expand the accent's functional reach without allowing other saturated colors to appear. End on a stable system state where the same accent maps connectivity or activation across the largest scale. The reusable mechanism is chromatic reservation, measurable scale ladder, single-source activation, visible carrier relay and network payoff—not the source frozen city, snow, giant humanoid, stone hand, tiny person, hood, golden eyes, molten roads, architecture, exact timing, creator identity or unsupported model mapping.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Reserve the world to one narrow low-saturation color family before any activation.
- Purpose: Creates perceptual capacity for one meaningful accent.
- Failure if removed: Multiple saturated colors turn the signal into decoration.

### inv-02

- Rule: Establish at least three measurable scales with persistent comparison anchors.
- Purpose: Makes the signal's expansion legible across size changes.
- Failure if removed: Abstract close-ups alone hide scale progression.

### inv-03

- Rule: Introduce exactly one saturated accent hue from one local source.
- Purpose: Assigns clear causal and visual priority.
- Failure if removed: Several activation hues divide attention and causality.

### inv-04

- Rule: Relay the same hue through a visible physical or informational carrier while earlier anchors remain recognizable.
- Purpose: Connects local activation to larger consequences.
- Failure if removed: Unmotivated color appearing elsewhere breaks continuity.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve the accent as a stable environment-scale network or system state with no new competing color.
- Purpose: Provides a functional terminal proof.
- Failure if removed: A final flash without mapped reach does not prove propagation.
