# 踏面异变｜重复触点到基底破裂 Template

## Mechanism

A support-surface transformation is earned by first proving a repeatable contact rule several times, then breaking that exact rule with one stronger contact. The camera must cross the same physical boundary so the lower environment reads as a consequence, and the ending should hold the new medium rather than immediately resetting.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one subject, one travel direction and one continuous support surface.
- Purpose: Defines the geometry whose rule will be tested.
- Failure if removed: Without a stable surface, later rupture feels arbitrary.

### inv-02

- Rule: Repeat the same localized response at multiple contact points.
- Purpose: Turns footfalls or touches into counted proof.
- Failure if removed: One decorative reaction cannot establish a rule.

### inv-03

- Rule: Increase response density while preserving subject identity and route.
- Purpose: Builds pressure without changing the premise.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated effects replace escalation with montage.

### inv-04

- Rule: Make one contact irreversibly breach the previously proven support rule.
- Purpose: Provides the causal transition.
- Failure if removed: A random cut below has no physical handoff.

### inv-05

- Rule: Carry the camera through the same boundary and hold the new medium.
- Purpose: Confirms spatial continuity and consequence.
- Failure if removed: Immediate reset erases the transformation.

## Transfer Tests

1. A lone fungal courier walks across a translucent pollen membrane in a greenhouse dome. Four footsteps send branching veins through the membrane; the fifth folds it inward and the courier descends into a glowing root chamber as the camera follows through the same tear.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family

Four passes leave directional ridge patterns rather than footprints; the fifth high-torque contact punctures the membrane, and a rotating inspection camera follows the sphere through the same opening into a lower induction-coil chamber.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, locomotion_mode, contact_geometry, setting, material_system, camera_grammar, breach_physics, lower_medium, sound_family
