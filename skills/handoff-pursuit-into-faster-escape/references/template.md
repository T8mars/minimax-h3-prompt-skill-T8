# 雨夜追逐｜街巷近战到机车脱身 Template

## Mechanism

Establish a readable pursuit corridor, interrupt running with one compact close encounter, then immediately complete a visible foot-to-faster-carrier handoff while preserving the established travel axis. Hold sustained acceleration on that axis; the source does not prove that the encounter creates the carrier or that pursuer distance increases on screen.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one readable pursuit axis before any combat.
- Purpose: Gives every later move a direction and stakes.
- Failure if removed: Action without geography becomes visual noise.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one narrow threshold to compress the lead and threat.
- Purpose: Creates a spatial reason for the encounter.
- Failure if removed: An arbitrary fight feels detached from the chase.

### inv-03

- Rule: Limit the close encounter to one clear block, counter and release.
- Purpose: Preserves chase momentum.
- Failure if removed: A long fight replaces the escape mechanism.

### inv-04

- Rule: Complete an immediate, visible foot-to-faster-carrier handoff after the encounter.
- Purpose: Keeps the chase continuous while changing the movement regime.
- Failure if removed: A delayed or disconnected carrier cut breaks the pursuit handoff.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with sustained carrier acceleration along the established travel direction.
- Purpose: Makes the new speed regime visible without inventing a measured gap.
- Failure if removed: A stationary carrier beauty shot does not complete the escape progression.
