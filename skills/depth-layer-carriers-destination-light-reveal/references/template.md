# 纵深载体引导目的光｜稳定结构中逐级扩大出口 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one enclosed original environment with a single distant destination light on a clear movement axis. Populate at least three depth planes with repeated slow carriers that share a visual family but vary scale and lateral drift. Move the camera continuously toward the same destination while fixed structural ribs, frames or columns pass as parallax and scale evidence. Let carriers intermittently cross the light without changing its location, and make the aperture grow monotonically until it becomes the dominant field while edge structures still prove continuity. The reusable mechanism is destination-locked traversal, layered slow carriers, fixed scale ribs, monotonic light growth and enclosure-to-release payoff—not the source cathedral, underwater setting, jellyfish, coral, moonlight, architecture, colors, exact camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one distant destination light and one unambiguous movement axis inside an enclosure.
- Purpose: Gives the traversal a measurable objective.
- Failure if removed: Multiple competing lights make progress unreadable.

### inv-02

- Rule: Place related slow carriers on at least three depth planes with different apparent speeds.
- Purpose: Makes parallax and scale visible without fast action.
- Failure if removed: One flat layer cannot communicate volume.

### inv-03

- Rule: Retain fixed ribs, frames or columns as orientation and scale anchors throughout the move.
- Purpose: Proves continuous travel through one space.
- Failure if removed: Removing all structure turns the sequence into an abstract dissolve.

### inv-04

- Rule: Grow the same destination light monotonically while carriers may briefly occlude it.
- Purpose: Creates a clear progress clock and anticipation.
- Failure if removed: A shrinking or relocated light breaks the destination contract.

### inv-05

- Rule: End when light dominates but leave edge anchors long enough to prove arrival from the original enclosure.
- Purpose: Delivers release without a disconnected white frame.
- Failure if removed: A hard unrelated cut loses the spatial payoff.
