# 帅气展示瞬间失手｜单次事故与社交收尾 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult performer, one original clearly adult familiar observer, a shared sightline and one simple open action area. Make the performer intentionally display competence through a continuous escalating movement phrase while the observer remains visibly attentive. Introduce exactly one readable physical mistake caused by the established movement, then show loss of balance, impact and immediate consequence without skipping the causal middle. Let the observer respond first with restrained concern or amusement, and let the performer complete one brief self-conscious recovery gesture that acknowledges the failed display. The reusable mechanism is witnessed self-presentation, escalating confidence, one causally legible mishap, consequence, social response and embarrassment recovery—not the source characters, faces, hair, clothing, franchise, flower grove, exact poses, fall choreography, composition, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly adult original roles, a shared sightline and the performer's deliberate competence-display intent before the risky move.
- Purpose: Makes the accident reverse a social goal rather than appear as random slapstick.
- Failure if removed: Without a witnessed display intention, the fall has no status reversal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Build one continuous movement phrase whose amplitude or difficulty visibly increases.
- Purpose: Creates a physical cause for overconfidence and keeps the setup compact.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated tricks make the eventual mistake arbitrary.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use exactly one mistake and show balance loss, impact and immediate consequence without a causal gap.
- Purpose: Preserves readable physics and concentrates the comic beat.
- Failure if removed: Multiple accidents or a cut directly to the ground weaken causality.

### inv-04

- Rule: Let the observer react before the performer begins the social recovery.
- Purpose: Makes the embarrassment depend on being seen.
- Failure if removed: If the performer recovers before the observer responds, the relationship contributes nothing.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on one restrained recovery gesture that acknowledges failure while both roles remain spatially connected.
- Purpose: Converts a physical gag into a reusable social closure.
- Failure if removed: Ending at impact leaves the relationship and emotional payoff unfinished.
