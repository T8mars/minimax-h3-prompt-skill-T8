# 背景异常｜先被观众发现再由记录设备证实 Template

## Mechanism

Place an anomaly in the background before foreground subjects notice it, preserve ordinary foreground behavior, use a recording or inspection device to isolate the anomaly as evidence, let the anomaly visibly disrupt or leave that evidence field, then delay the subjects' reaction until after the proof changes.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Reveal the anomaly to the viewer before the subjects notice it.
- Purpose: Creates dramatic information asymmetry.
- Failure if removed: Simultaneous discovery removes suspense.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep foreground behavior ordinary during the first anomaly state.
- Purpose: Makes the background disruption legible.
- Failure if removed: Immediate panic spends the escalation.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one device or inspection frame to isolate the anomaly.
- Purpose: Turns suspicion into observable evidence.
- Failure if removed: A random close-up does not prove discovery.

### inv-04

- Rule: Change the anomaly inside or against the evidence field.
- Purpose: Makes the proof active rather than static.
- Failure if removed: An unchanged recording adds no causal turn.

### inv-05

- Rule: Delay the human reaction until after the proof changes.
- Purpose: Completes the viewer-subject information handoff.
- Failure if removed: Early reaction breaks the asymmetry.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| causal_carrier | One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain. |
| state_change | Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change. |
