# 冲击后揭示｜从私密困境到城市尺度真因 Template

## Mechanism

Begin inside a tight protected or confined relationship, let one immense external force abruptly displace the carrier, prove impact and disorientation locally, hold a survivor reaction long enough to register consequence, then widen to reveal the much larger environmental cause and aftermath.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Begin with an intimate confined baseline before the large event.
- Purpose: Creates a human scale for the later force.
- Failure if removed: Opening on destruction removes scale contrast.

### inv-02

- Rule: Let one external force visibly displace the established carrier.
- Purpose: Makes the escalation causal.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated disaster inserts do not move the story.

### inv-03

- Rule: Prove impact through local physical aftermath.
- Purpose: Closes the displacement event.
- Failure if removed: Skipping impact makes the carrier teleport.

### inv-04

- Rule: Hold one post-impact reaction after motion stops.
- Purpose: Gives the consequence time to register.
- Failure if removed: Cutting immediately to spectacle loses the human cost.

### inv-05

- Rule: Reveal the larger cause only after local consequence is clear.
- Purpose: Recontextualizes the opening scale.
- Failure if removed: An early wide reveal spends the final information too soon.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| causal_carrier | One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain. |
| state_change | Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Begin with an intimate confined baseline before the large event.
- Let one external force visibly displace the established carrier.
- Hold one post-impact reaction after motion stops.
- Reveal the larger cause only after local consequence is clear.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source woman and automobile
- burning giant machines and falling wreckage
- source street destruction and source end card
- the exact trapped-car crash storyboard
- STILL HUMAN end card
- WATCH CHAPTER ONE ON YOUTUBE

## Transfer Tests

1. A lone glass conservator works inside a compact museum restoration pod; a pressure wave slides the whole pod across a flooded hall, local cracked seals prove impact, the conservator steadies, and a final wide reveals a ruptured overhead aquarium.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A seed technician tends one capsule inside a narrow orbital greenhouse bay; a magnetic surge drags the bay along its rail, loose soil and harness tension prove the stop, and a late exterior reveals a vast solar-sail deployment bending the station.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
