# 图形赛道｜穿越圆门、障碍升级与单人收束 Template

## Mechanism

A single color-locked lead travels through a graphic environment that behaves as a course: oversized planar signs, directional graphic gates, spatial reset, denser multi-object obstacle interval and solo final poster. Preserve progressive route and graphic-to-spatial coupling, not the source typography, outfit or violence.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Identify one lead with a persistent silhouette/accent color before moving through the oversized graphic course.
- Purpose: Keeps spatial changes trackable.
- Failure if removed: Without one stable lead, rapid zones seem unrelated.

### inv-02

- Rule: Make two or more graphic structures function as traversable space and use one circular gate as a visible transition.
- Purpose: Gives the course a specific graphic-motion grammar.
- Failure if removed: If graphics remain mere overlays, travel lacks environmental stakes.

### inv-03

- Rule: After a short identity reset, raise density with multiple obstacles and then isolate the lead in a held final frame.
- Purpose: Provides escalation and decisive visual closure.
- Failure if removed: Without reset and isolation, the climax becomes uninterrupted action noise.

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 主体身份跨空间不变
- 图形成为真实可走结构
- 终点结果与单人姿态稳定

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- No source title, wordmark, exact English letterform walls or typography layout.
- No source character face, hair, red trousers, fight poses or opponent designs.
- Do not imply original source sound or precise portal lore was verified.
