# 弱势反攻清场｜贴地恢复、水盾承压到环形冲击 Template

## Mechanism

Place one original clearly adult protagonist at a measurable disadvantage inside a ring of adult opponents. The recovery must progress through four distinct but materially continuous ability states: a grounded positional escape, a compact guard that visibly deforms under one close hit, a focused counter that uses the retained material, and one floor-bound radial release that moves opponents outward. Preserve the same water, light, dust or other medium between stages and keep bodies readable through every effect. End on a quiet center-versus-ring tableau. The reusable mechanism is disadvantage baseline, grounded recovery, deforming guard, retained-material counter, radial clearing proof and calm center—not the source school setting, youthful styling, people, uniforms, water design, exact choreography, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Use clearly adult original characters in a non-school setting and establish a measurable center-versus-ring disadvantage.
- Purpose: Removes ambiguous age and makes the reversal spatially legible.
- Failure if removed: Without adult identity and group geometry, the sequence becomes unsafe or abstract.

### inv-02

- Rule: Begin recovery with one grounded positional action before any large ability.
- Purpose: Gives the protagonist earned space and a physical first step.
- Failure if removed: Immediate spectacle skips the disadvantage-to-agency transition.

### inv-03

- Rule: Make one compact guard visibly deform under a close committed hit while both bodies remain readable.
- Purpose: Proves defense rather than decorative shielding.
- Failure if removed: An opaque bubble hides contact and consequence.

### inv-04

- Rule: Reuse the same retained medium for the focused counter and radial release.
- Purpose: Creates material continuity across ability stages.
- Failure if removed: Fresh unrelated effects feel like a disconnected power reel.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with opponents displaced outward and the same adult calm at the unchanged center.
- Purpose: Provides geometric proof of the clear.
- Failure if removed: A close-up ending cannot verify the radial result.
