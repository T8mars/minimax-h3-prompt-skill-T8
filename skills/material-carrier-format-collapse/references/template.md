# 材质引路｜空间生长到版式定格 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with one small material guide aligned to a stable subject. Keep that same material visually continuous as it pulls the camera, accumulates color and depth, becomes one readable physical structure, and expands into an inhabitable environment. Reserve one final camera rise that compresses the dimensional world into a held graphic sheet while preserving the subject and material history.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Introduce one small material guide on the same optical axis as a stable subject.
- Purpose: Creates a precise origin for the transformation.
- Failure if removed: Without a small origin and stable subject, the sequence starts as an ungrounded effects montage.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep one continuous material family responsible for camera guidance, color accumulation and spatial growth.
- Purpose: Makes every phase feel causally related.
- Failure if removed: Changing material rules between phases breaks the transformation chain.

### inv-03

- Rule: Let the material form one readable, depth-bearing structure inside the scene and hold it briefly.
- Purpose: Provides a recognition checkpoint before scale expansion.
- Failure if removed: Flat overlay copy or unreadable decoration cannot prove physical integration.

### inv-04

- Rule: Expand the same material into an environment that still contains the original subject and prior palette.
- Purpose: Turns a local effect into a world-scale payoff without losing identity.
- Failure if removed: A disconnected new room would read as a scene swap.

### inv-05

- Rule: Use one late camera rise to resolve the dimensional scene into a held graphic sheet that preserves subject and material history.
- Purpose: Closes spatial spectacle with an inspectable format change.
- Failure if removed: An arbitrary end card would not prove continuity with the preceding world.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |
