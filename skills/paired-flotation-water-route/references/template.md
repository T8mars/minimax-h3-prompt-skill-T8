# 双脚浮具水面路线｜交替承重、连续尾流与远端抵达 Template

## Mechanism

固定同一成年人物、码头、水面和远方地标，先证明双脚分别绑定独立透明浮具；人物以左右脚交替承重离开码头，每次接触都产生可归因圆波和连续尾流，短暂回看后继续沿同一方向远离并接近既定目标。 The reusable mechanism preserves causal order, spatial anchors and the final proof while replacing every source-specific person, location, object, wording, choreography and design.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: One clearly adult identity, one departure threshold, one distant destination and two separately bound flotation devices establish the baseline.
- Purpose: Establishes the baseline identity, object or geography that every later state must preserve.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact baseline rule, later changes cannot be attributed to the same subject, object or place, so the sequence loses its reference state. Removed rule: "One clearly adult identity, one departure threshold, one distant destination and two separately bound flotation devices establish the baseline."

### inv-02

- Rule: Every step assigns weight to one visible device before a corresponding ripple appears.
- Purpose: Makes the visible trigger precede and explain the first mechanism response.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact trigger rule, the response appears spontaneously and the central cause-and-effect claim is no longer demonstrated. Removed rule: "Every step assigns weight to one visible device before a corresponding ripple appears."

### inv-03

- Rule: The two devices remain distinct and generate two traceable wake lines throughout the route.
- Purpose: Keeps the middle progression registered instead of allowing an unexplained reset.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact progression rule, the middle can jump to a replacement state and the accumulated result is no longer traceable. Removed rule: "The two devices remain distinct and generate two traceable wake lines throughout the route."

### inv-04

- Rule: A single look-back may interrupt gaze but cannot reverse travel direction or reset geography.
- Purpose: Reconnects the changed or partial state to the established subject and spatial anchor.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact reconnection rule, detail or transformed shots can belong to a different subject or geography and continuity cannot be verified. Removed rule: "A single look-back may interrupt gaze but cannot reverse travel direction or reset geography."

### inv-05

- Rule: The final wide retains adult, departure landmark, destination axis and continuous wake proof.
- Purpose: Turns the terminal hold into observable proof that the mechanism completed.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact terminal-proof rule, the sequence stops on motion alone and never confirms the promised outcome or preserved aftermath. Removed rule: "The final wide retains adult, departure landmark, destination axis and continuous wake proof."
