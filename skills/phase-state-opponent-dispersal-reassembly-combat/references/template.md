# 相态敌手攻防｜实体命中、粒子卸力与反向重组 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult physical protagonist and one original phase-changing opponent on a stable attack axis before close combat begins. Bind every decisive contact to a directional material response: the opponent must visibly transition from coherent silhouette into strands, particles or fluid-like mass at the contact point, keep that displaced material inside the shared geography, then rebuild from it rather than respawning. Escalate from local deformation to full-body dispersal while preserving ground, light direction and subject positions as continuity anchors. End after the apparent terminal burst by reassembling the opponent in a previously established zone. The reusable mechanism is contact-driven phase change, retained material continuity, escalating dispersal scale and post-victory reassembly—not the source fighters, masks, costumes, weapons, desert, dust palette, choreography, framing, watermark, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish both clearly adult subjects, one ground plane and one attack axis before the first close contact.
- Purpose: Creates spatial proof for later deformation and recovery.
- Failure if removed: Without a stable baseline, particle motion cannot be tied to either combatant.

### inv-02

- Rule: Bind every decisive hit to a directional phase change beginning at the visible contact point.
- Purpose: Makes the supernatural response a physical consequence of action.
- Failure if removed: A detached effect cloud reads as decoration rather than damage or defense.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep displaced strands or particles visible until they reconnect into the same opponent.
- Purpose: Preserves material conservation and identity continuity.
- Failure if removed: A clean disappearance followed by a fresh body reads as a reset.

### inv-04

- Rule: Escalate from local deformation to at least one full-body dispersal while retaining ground and light anchors.
- Purpose: Raises threat scale without losing combat geography.
- Failure if removed: Random spectacle or location changes break the progression.

### inv-05

- Rule: After an apparent terminal burst, reassemble the opponent inside a previously established zone.
- Purpose: Turns phase control into the final narrative proof.
- Failure if removed: Ending before reassembly reduces the mechanism to ordinary defeat.
