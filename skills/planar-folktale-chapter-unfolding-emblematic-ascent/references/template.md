# 平面民间故事展开｜章节换景与月轮升格 Template

## Mechanism

Build one original planar handmade-story stage with a persistent material grammar, one grounded discoverer, one sealed luminous container, one central adult figure and one celestial destination. Let the discoverer approach and open the container so the central figure unfolds from the same material. Reconfigure the existing layers into a community chapter where at least two witnesses bring symbolic objects that establish social recognition. Accumulate those objects, then use one controlled full-frame material rupture to expose the night layer and previously recoverable celestial goal. Carry the same adult figure upward along a visible material path while ground witnesses remain legible, and end in an emblematic wide that contains the destination plus at least two earlier spatial anchors. The reusable mechanism is planar discovery, material birth, chapter-fold transition, social recognition, material rupture, vertical departure and total-story tableau—not the source Kaguya-hime identity, Japanese folktale, bamboo, garments, gifts, house, moon composition, silhouettes, exact paper craft, color sequence, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one coherent planar material grammar, a discoverer, sealed luminous container, central adult figure and celestial destination system.
- Purpose: Gives every later transformation shared spatial and material rules.
- Failure if removed: Without a common stage and goal system, the chapters become unrelated illustrations.

### inv-02

- Rule: Open the sealed object and unfold the central figure from that same material without replacing identity.
- Purpose: Makes the protagonist's arrival physically causal.
- Failure if removed: Cutting directly to a person removes the discovery-to-birth handoff.

### inv-03

- Rule: Reconfigure existing layers into a community chapter where at least two witnesses deliver symbolic objects.
- Purpose: Moves the story from private discovery to visible social recognition.
- Failure if removed: A solitary pose cannot establish the community phase.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use accumulated objects in one controlled material rupture that exposes the night layer and celestial goal.
- Purpose: Turns social abundance into the physical cause of departure.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated sky cut breaks the chapter transition.

### inv-05

- Rule: Show the same adult figure ascend along a visible material path and finish wide with the destination, ground witnesses and at least two prior setting anchors.
- Purpose: Preserves identity and closes the entire story geography.
- Failure if removed: An isolated moon portrait cannot prove departure from the established world.
