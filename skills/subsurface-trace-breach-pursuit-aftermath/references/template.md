# 地下轨迹破土｜尺度反转、追逐穿越与空场余波 Template

## Mechanism

Establish at least one original clearly adult moving subject inside an ordinary route with three stable scale landmarks. Introduce one attributable subsurface trace that travels in a readable direction relative to the subject and escalates through at least two observable ground states before any body is visible. Keep the trace, breach point and subject in one geography when an original large entity emerges; hold them together long enough to prove scale, then let the entity cross or pursue along the established route without teleportation. End after the moving agents leave the immediate frame and hold on persistent dust, displaced material or broken surface plus the original landmarks. The reusable mechanism is ordinary scale baseline, directional hidden warning, staged ground escalation, same-space breach, landmark-based scale proof and aftermath hold—not the source adults, faces, clothing, giant ant, rural road, tanks, desert, genre or decade reference, exact actions, camera shake, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish at least one clearly adult moving subject and three stable route landmarks before the ground anomaly begins.
- Purpose: Creates a human-scale baseline and recoverable geography for the later breach.
- Failure if removed: Without ordinary landmarks, the entity's size and continuity cannot be verified.

### inv-02

- Rule: Move one subsurface trace in a readable direction and escalate it through at least two ground states before emergence.
- Purpose: Builds warning and causality from hidden motion instead of a surprise cut.
- Failure if removed: An instant creature appearance removes the trace-to-breach mechanism.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep subject, trace and breach point in one geography when the original large entity emerges.
- Purpose: Proves that the visible body is the cause of the prior ground disturbance.
- Failure if removed: A cut to a separate creature shot can imply an unrelated event.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use the adult and at least two established landmarks to prove scale while the entity crosses or pursues along the same route.
- Purpose: Makes the scale reversal measurable and keeps action spatially continuous.
- Failure if removed: A tight monster close-up may look large without demonstrating its relation to the route.

### inv-05

- Rule: After the moving agents exit, hold persistent displaced material and the original landmarks.
- Purpose: Shows that the breach materially changed the established world.
- Failure if removed: Resetting to a clean road erases the consequence and weakens closure.
