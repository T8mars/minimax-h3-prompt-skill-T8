# 非对称突破｜密集阻力到空间制度改变 Template

## Mechanism

Establish a fortified objective and dense resisting field, introduce one asymmetric moving force, prove momentum through several local contacts, widen to show progress toward the objective, then use one breach to change the spatial regime before a close held consequence.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one fixed objective and a dense visible resistance field.
- Purpose: Makes progress measurable.
- Failure if removed: Unanchored action lacks direction.

### inv-02

- Rule: Introduce one asymmetric force with a clear motion axis.
- Purpose: Creates a readable imbalance.
- Failure if removed: Several equal forces create generic chaos.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use multiple local contacts to prove momentum.
- Purpose: Turns scale into physical evidence.
- Failure if removed: A distant charge alone does not prove force.

### inv-04

- Rule: Return wide to show progress toward the boundary.
- Purpose: Connects contacts to objective change.
- Failure if removed: Only close action hides whether progress occurred.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let one breach change the spatial regime and hold the consequence.
- Purpose: Provides an irreversible payoff.
- Failure if removed: Ending before entry leaves the objective unresolved.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| causal_carrier | One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain. |
| state_change | Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Let one breach change the spatial regime and hold the consequence.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source fantasy beast and armored army
- castle gate, siege field and fire
- the exact creature charge and breach choreography
- source medieval costume and weapon design

## Transfer Tests

1. In a flooded logistics hall, one heavy autonomous tug crosses a dense field of floating foam barriers, proves leverage through three contacts, reaches a sealed relief corridor and pushes one gate open so rescue skiffs can pass.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. In a paper theater, one sustained air pulse travels radially through a dense ring of folded curtains, proves force through three successive unfurling contacts, then opens a projection wall through a paper iris so the space changes from a storage maze into one continuous performance field.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
