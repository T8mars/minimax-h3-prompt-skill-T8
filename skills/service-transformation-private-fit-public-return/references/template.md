# 服务改造｜私域工序到公开身份回归 Template

## Mechanism

Introduce one adult client with a visibly inadequate, damaged or misfitting state and bring them into a clearly defined professional service space. Show the specialist assessing the problem and using at least two visible tools or manual interventions. Preserve the client's identity and body registration through a staged transformation. Require both professional fit verification and one self-directed adjustment by the client, then return the client to a public context where changed posture, movement and observer response prove the result. The reusable mechanism is deficient baseline, professional assessment, attributable craft, identity-preserving transformation, dual fit confirmation and public return—not the source animated characters, body proportions, ragged coat, tailor, shop, red thread, pin, navy suit, street crowd, exact action, studio style, creator identity or model claim.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish an adult client's visible baseline problem inside or immediately before a professional service space.
- Purpose: Defines what the service must change.
- Failure if removed: An unproven problem makes the makeover ornamental.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show specialist assessment plus at least two visible tools or manual interventions.
- Purpose: Attributes the outcome to professional craft.
- Failure if removed: A magical cut without process removes service causality.

### inv-03

- Rule: Preserve client identity and body registration through a staged transformation.
- Purpose: Makes the before-and-after comparison trustworthy.
- Failure if removed: Replacing the body or angle breaks continuity.

### inv-04

- Rule: Include professional fit verification and one self-directed client adjustment.
- Purpose: Proves both external quality and internal acceptance.
- Failure if removed: Only admiration leaves usability untested.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return to a public context where posture, movement and at least one observer response register the change.
- Purpose: Converts private craft into functional and social payoff.
- Failure if removed: Ending in the workshop withholds consequence.

   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
