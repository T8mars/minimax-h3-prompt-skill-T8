# 微型载体FPV产品回环｜桌面障碍、生态穿越与包装复归 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original micro-scale flying carrier, one product anchor and a human-scale environment in the same frame so relative size is immediate. Maintain a continuous forward FPV route through at least three obstacle chapters that change navigation rule—such as low tabletop weave, soft environmental corridor and enclosed organic tunnel—while every chapter retains a visible exit gap. Use one repeated shape, color or opening to redirect the carrier back toward the original product geography. Decelerate before the product return, then let the carrier land or hover in a stable relation to the package before one restrained product-state effect and concise name lockup. The reusable mechanism is micro scale, continuous FPV, changing obstacle grammar, visible exits, motif-guided return, deceleration and product reunion—not the source bee, picnic, people, child, dog, foods, flowers, fruit, cans, branding, exact route, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Show one micro carrier, one product and human-scale objects together before flight.
- Purpose: Makes scale and terminal objective measurable.
- Failure if removed: Starting in abstract flight loses product context.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use at least three forward obstacle chapters with different navigation rules and visible exits.
- Purpose: Creates progression without disorienting the route.
- Failure if removed: Identical weaving becomes repetitive; hidden exits feel random.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep carrier identity, heading logic and near-miss clearance physically continuous.
- Purpose: Makes speed thrilling but readable.
- Failure if removed: Teleporting or intersecting objects breaks FPV credibility.

### inv-04

- Rule: Repeat one shape, color or opening to redirect the route toward original product geography.
- Purpose: Turns the return into designed recall rather than coincidence.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated cut back to the package breaks the journey.

### inv-05

- Rule: Decelerate, reunite carrier and product, then use one restrained effect and concise name.
- Purpose: Lets motion resolve into a legible commercial payoff.
- Failure if removed: A high-speed logo collision makes the product unreadable.
