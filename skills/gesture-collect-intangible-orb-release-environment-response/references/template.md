# 手势收集无形现象｜凝聚成球后释放并让环境回应 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult subject moving through an ordinary environment with both hands visibly empty. Let the adult stop and shape one stable collection volume with both palms before any visible phenomenon appears. Draw fine related traces from at least three surrounding environmental locations toward that volume, keep them materially continuous, and compress them into one single manipulable core. Use exactly one release gesture to send the same core away from the hands along an established spatial axis. Expand that material back into the environment as a distributed, readable response while the original location and adult remain identifiable. The reusable mechanism is empty-hand baseline, gesture-defined collection volume, environmental convergence, single-core compression, one release and distributed world response—not the source man, face, clothing, street, light strands, orb design, city, trees, exact hand motion, camera, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Show one adult, ordinary environment and visibly empty hands before collection.
- Purpose: Creates proof that the phenomenon is acquired rather than pre-held.
- Failure if removed: An existing prop removes the intangible-to-tangible conversion.

### inv-02

- Rule: Form one stable two-hand collection volume before traces appear.
- Purpose: Gives invisible material a spatial destination.
- Failure if removed: Changing hand shapes make convergence unreadable.

### inv-03

- Rule: Pull continuous related traces from at least three environmental sources into one core.
- Purpose: Binds the material to the world and shows accumulation.
- Failure if removed: Particles appearing only at the palms look conjured without collection.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use exactly one release gesture and preserve the core's material identity along one axis.
- Purpose: Makes the reversal causal and trackable.
- Failure if removed: Multiple throws or an unrelated object break conservation.

### inv-05

- Rule: Distribute the released material back across the same environment and hold the response.
- Purpose: Completes the collection-release cycle at world scale.
- Failure if removed: Ending on the orb alone leaves no environmental consequence.
