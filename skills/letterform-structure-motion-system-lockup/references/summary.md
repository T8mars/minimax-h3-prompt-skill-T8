# 字形结构运动系统｜组件组字、分栏、物理碰撞与双行锁定 — 摘要

## 用途

用首个标题的字形组件生成分栏与运动骨架，后续短词分别承担冲击、网格、卡片与碰撞动作；限定调色和文字集合，最终所有层同时停止在双行锁定。

## 推荐输入格式

`Declare a small exact text set, one restricted palette and one primary title before animation begins. + Build the primary title from visible components and reuse one of its letterform structures as the layout carrier. + Give each secondary word one distinct readable physical action inside the inherited structure. + Compress prior cards or grids into one central collision while preserving exact spelling. + Resolve all fragments into one complete terminal lockup and stop background, decoration and foreground together.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 只用米白、墨黑和一种钴蓝点色；散落线段向中心旋入并组装为准确主标题【NORTH LAB】。；轨道内只引入准确短词【ORBIT】【FIELD】【SIGNAL】：ORBIT 环绕一次，FIELD 在透视网格上复制，SIGNAL 正面撞入并停住。；碎片清场后把准确【LAB】置于准确【NORTH】上方，中间一条钴蓝横线；背景、刻度与前景同时停住1.2秒。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- Declare a small exact text set, one restricted palette and one primary title before animation begins.
- Build the primary title from visible components and reuse one of its letterform structures as the layout carrier.
- Compress prior cards or grids into one central collision while preserving exact spelling.
- Resolve all fragments into one complete terminal lockup and stop background, decoration and foreground together.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变人物、环境、道具、风格、声音与结局表面，禁止只换名词。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
