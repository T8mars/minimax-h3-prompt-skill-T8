# 字形结构运动系统｜组件组字、分栏、物理碰撞与双行锁定 Template

## Mechanism

Declare a small exact text set, one restricted palette and one primary title before animation begins. Build the primary title from visible components and reuse one of its letterform structures as the layout carrier. Give each secondary word one distinct readable physical action inside the inherited structure. Compress prior cards or grids into one central collision while preserving exact spelling. Resolve all fragments into one complete terminal lockup and stop background, decoration and foreground together. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Declare a small exact text set, one restricted palette and one primary title before animation begins.
- Purpose: Makes the baseline measurable before the mechanism changes it.
- Failure if removed: Without the baseline, later change has no trustworthy reference state.

### inv-02

- Rule: Build the primary title from visible components and reuse one of its letterform structures as the layout carrier.
- Purpose: Binds the first visible change to a specific cause rather than decoration.
- Failure if removed: Without a visible trigger, the transition reads as an arbitrary edit.

### inv-03

- Rule: Give each secondary word one distinct readable physical action inside the inherited structure.
- Purpose: Preserves identity, material or spatial continuity through the middle transition.
- Failure if removed: Without continuity, the sequence becomes unrelated montage or identity drift.

### inv-04

- Rule: Compress prior cards or grids into one central collision while preserving exact spelling.
- Purpose: Raises intensity or information while retaining the same readable grammar.
- Failure if removed: Without controlled escalation, the middle either plateaus or turns into noise.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve all fragments into one complete terminal lockup and stop background, decoration and foreground together.
- Purpose: Provides a terminal proof that resolves the mechanism instead of merely stopping.
- Failure if removed: Without terminal proof, the promised result or reversal remains unverified.
