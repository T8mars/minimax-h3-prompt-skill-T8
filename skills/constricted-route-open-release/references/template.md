# 窄路追逐｜连续压缩后豁然释放 Template

## Mechanism

Lock a fast carrier and its camera to one continuous constrained route. Escalate by reducing clearance from different sides while preserving forward direction and recurring route geometry. Make one final tunnel or aperture the tightest compression, then cross it exactly once into a materially wider environment where near obstacles disappear and the camera finally slows enough to prove the release.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one fast carrier, forward route and coupled camera before the first hard constriction.
- Purpose: Creates stable pursuit geography.
- Failure if removed: A late carrier reveal makes clearance impossible to judge.

### inv-02

- Rule: Reduce usable clearance progressively with side, overhead and route obstacles.
- Purpose: Builds pressure through geometry rather than random cuts.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated hazards feel episodic.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep carrier and camera on the same continuous direction through each opening.
- Purpose: Preserves kinetic continuity.
- Failure if removed: Axis resets turn the sequence into disconnected action shots.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use one final tunnel or aperture as the narrowest visible compression.
- Purpose: Creates a measurable release threshold.
- Failure if removed: Several equivalent exits dilute the peak.

### inv-05

- Rule: Cross once into an obstacle-free large environment and let the camera widen or slow.
- Purpose: Makes openness the terminal payoff.
- Failure if removed: Continuing dense hazards after the exit cancels the release.
