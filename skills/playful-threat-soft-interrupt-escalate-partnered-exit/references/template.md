# 威胁动作被亲昵打断｜升级再打断后转成双人撤离 Template

## Mechanism

Establish two original clearly adult characters with an already-legible familiar relationship on one continuous path. Let one adult introduce a controlled prop-based threat that stays physically separated from faces and bodies, then have the partner cross its dramatic meaning with exactly one brief non-harmful interpersonal interruption. Show the threatened adult's surprise before escalating to one larger but still safe movement; the partner responds with a second distinct soft interruption rather than force. Preserve costume, prop, path direction and personal boundaries throughout. End when both adults voluntarily align to the same travel direction and leave together, reframing conflict as bonded play. The reusable mechanism is controlled threat, soft semantic interruption, reaction, safe escalation, repeated relational override and partnered exit—not the source woman, man, faces, robes, sword, bamboo forest, finger tap, sleeves, kicks, exact gestures, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly adult original characters, a familiar relationship and one continuous shared path.
- Purpose: Makes later intimacy and partnered exit consensually legible.
- Failure if removed: Unknown or minor-coded characters make soft contact unsafe or ambiguous.

### inv-02

- Rule: Introduce one controlled prop threat with clear physical separation from bodies and faces.
- Purpose: Creates dramatic tension without depicting actual harm.
- Failure if removed: Contact with the weapon changes the scene into violence.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one brief non-harmful interpersonal interruption and hold the threatened adult's reaction.
- Purpose: Lets relationship meaning override action meaning.
- Failure if removed: Skipping the reaction makes the touch decorative.

### inv-04

- Rule: Escalate once through a larger safe movement, then answer with a second distinct soft interruption.
- Purpose: Creates a comic two-turn pattern rather than random flirting.
- Failure if removed: Repeated identical beats feel mechanical; real force breaks the tone.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with both adults voluntarily aligned and moving together along the established path.
- Purpose: Reframes conflict as bonded play through action.
- Failure if removed: One character fleeing alone would imply unresolved threat.
