# 接触触发尺度生长｜小型陪伴物入介质后长成巨体 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one clearly adult original person, one small nonhuman companion and measurable environment scale. Use one visible contact with a declared medium as the sole transformation trigger. Keep recognizable companion features and displaced medium continuously visible during growth. Make environment reaction scale with body growth while preserving the adult's position and geography. End with the same adult and transformed companion in one readable frame that proves final scale. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult original person, one small nonhuman companion and measurable environment scale.
- Purpose: Makes the baseline measurable before the mechanism changes it.
- Failure if removed: Without the baseline, later change has no trustworthy reference state.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one visible contact with a declared medium as the sole transformation trigger.
- Purpose: Binds the first visible change to a specific cause rather than decoration.
- Failure if removed: Without a visible trigger, the transition reads as an arbitrary edit.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep recognizable companion features and displaced medium continuously visible during growth.
- Purpose: Preserves identity, material or spatial continuity through the middle transition.
- Failure if removed: Without continuity, the sequence becomes unrelated montage or identity drift.

### inv-04

- Rule: Make environment reaction scale with body growth while preserving the adult's position and geography.
- Purpose: Raises intensity or information while retaining the same readable grammar.
- Failure if removed: Without controlled escalation, the middle either plateaus or turns into noise.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with the same adult and transformed companion in one readable frame that proves final scale.
- Purpose: Provides a terminal proof that resolves the mechanism instead of merely stopping.
- Failure if removed: Without terminal proof, the promised result or reversal remains unverified.
