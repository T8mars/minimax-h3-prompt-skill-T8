# 中心触发｜径向环境变形与余波 Template

## Mechanism

A single center-anchored action converts a one-versus-many scale problem into a large radial restructuring of the existing environment. The outward boundary must precede physical geometry change, and the aftermath must retain the actor at the original center while surrounding objects remain spatially accountable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Show one clearly located central actor or initiator against a much larger distributed opposing field before activation.
- Purpose: Scale establishes the problem and fixes the origin.
- Failure if removed: Without a stable center and surrounding field, radial consequence has no readable before state.

### inv-02

- Rule: One physical contact or control action visibly starts an outward radial signal; existing terrain/structure changes only after that signal reaches it.
- Purpose: Preserves cause before effect and direction of propagation.
- Failure if removed: Random cuts or pre-existing deformation break the actor-to-world causal chain.

### inv-03

- Rule: Finish with a wide view that retains the original center and proves a changed large-scale geometry around it.
- Purpose: Makes the action's consequence measurable by composition.
- Failure if removed: A close-up ending or swapped geography leaves the structural result unproven.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| initiator | One stable center actor/controller with a visible single trigger. |
| distributed_field | A much larger visible group or hazard before activation; avoid source army. |
| terrain_system | Existing floor or landscape with physical material continuity. |
| propagation | One outward-moving boundary preceding each structural change. |
| geometry_result | Large-scale radial structure demonstrably different at the end. |
| camera | Before wide scale, close trigger, overhead progression, wide result. |
| sound | Original sound may track trigger and progressive structure; no source audio attribution. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 中心主体锚定
- 传播先于几何变化
- 终态仍可追溯原空间

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- No wizard, staff, armored army, siege beasts, gold rune circle, dark-fantasy battlefield or copied sky treatment.
- Do not reproduce the source exact crater shot or creator's long live-page prompt as if it were hash-bound prompt evidence.
- No unexplained destruction of people or buildings; new structures and affected objects remain physically accountable.
