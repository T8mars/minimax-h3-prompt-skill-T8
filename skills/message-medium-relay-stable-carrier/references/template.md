# 信息接力｜稳定载体穿越多种实体媒介 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one stable carrier and the first concise readable message state, then advance a related sequence of short messages through at least three materially different physical or graphical media. Each new phrase must progress the informational state rather than repeat arbitrary slogans, every handoff must be motivated by carrier movement, contact or an explicit spatial transition, and each medium must demonstrate distinct physics rather than only a new font. Vary camera duty to prove those physics, preserve the carrier or its consequence, and finish on a clear terminal message in a new medium without returning to the opening state. Source wording, performer design and exact media order are not reusable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one stable carrier and the first concise original message state before the first medium change.
- Purpose: Defines the identity and informational starting point of the relay.
- Failure if removed: Without a stable carrier and initial message state, later scenes become unrelated typography shots.

### inv-02

- Rule: Advance a related sequence of short messages through at least three media with visibly different material behavior.
- Purpose: Proves a coherent informational progression and cross-medium continuity.
- Failure if removed: Repeating one word without progression, or swapping only color and font, does not form the observed evolving-message relay.

### inv-03

- Rule: Make each handoff causal through carrier movement, contact or a readable spatial boundary.
- Purpose: Connects the media changes into one forward process.
- Failure if removed: Arbitrary montage cuts remove the sense of transfer.

### inv-04

- Rule: Change camera duty to prove each medium's physics while keeping the carrier or consequence traceable.
- Purpose: Makes material differences inspectable without losing continuity.
- Failure if removed: Repeating one frontal beauty frame hides whether the media behave differently.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve the final message state in a new terminal medium and hold it without returning to the opening state.
- Purpose: Gives the informational relay an irreversible finish.
- Failure if removed: A loop back to the first treatment or an unheld slogan collapses the forward progression.
