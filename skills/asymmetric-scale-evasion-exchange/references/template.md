# 巨体攻防｜共享空间中的连续闪避 Template

## Mechanism

An asymmetric-scale exchange is readable when one large body repeatedly commits slow, space-consuming movements and a smaller body answers with precise route changes through visible negative space. Continuous shared geography, not impact spectacle, proves the tactical relationship, and the ending must not claim a victory the image does not show.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish the full scale difference and a common ground plane before the first exchange.
- Purpose: Lets viewers judge mass and available space.
- Failure if removed: Tight fragments hide the central asymmetry.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give the large body one slow committed movement that visibly occupies a route.
- Purpose: Creates a readable spatial problem.
- Failure if removed: Random flailing produces noise instead of pressure.

### inv-03

- Rule: Answer with a smaller subject's route change through a visible gap rather than equal-force collision.
- Purpose: Makes agility, not implausible strength, the counter-rule.
- Failure if removed: A head-on match erases the scale premise.

### inv-04

- Rule: Preserve both participants and the ground axis through each recovery.
- Purpose: Maintains causal and spatial continuity.
- Failure if removed: Reframing either participant out breaks the exchange logic.

### inv-05

- Rule: Hold the final shared state without inventing defeat, rescue or alliance.
- Purpose: Keeps the source-derived mechanism evidence-bounded.
- Failure if removed: An unsupported win condition overstates the visible ending.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- light-clothed human fighter
- large dark rock-like humanoid
- misty wet shoreline plane
- source circling combat blocking
- source body proportions and attack choreography

## Transfer Tests

1. A palm-sized inspection rover crosses a bright paper mill floor while a slow overhead bale arm sweeps across marked lanes. The rover waits, slips through a visible gap, pivots under a second sweep and ends facing the still-active arm in one continuous lateral shot.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A small ceramic crab moves across a circular tide laboratory while a massive rotating water gate closes broad sectors. An overhead camera keeps the ring geometry visible as the crab changes lanes through three shrinking openings and reaches a neutral holding zone without defeating the gate.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
