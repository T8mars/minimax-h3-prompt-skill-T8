# 护送穿行｜关系确认到发光门槛 Template

## Mechanism

Establish a protector-dependent relationship before movement, show the protector choose and lead one continuous route while maintaining readable contact, preserve both identities through the environment, then cross one visually distinct threshold together and hold their connection.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish the protector and dependent role through visible contact before travel.
- Purpose: Defines who leads and why.
- Failure if removed: Starting in motion obscures the relationship.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show the protector choose or confirm one route before escorting.
- Purpose: Makes movement goal-directed.
- Failure if removed: An unexplained cut to travel removes agency.

### inv-03

- Rule: Maintain readable physical or spatial guidance through the route.
- Purpose: Proves escort behavior.
- Failure if removed: Independent walking weakens protection.

### inv-04

- Rule: Keep both identities and recurring landmarks traceable.
- Purpose: Preserves continuity.
- Failure if removed: Losing one subject breaks the route.

### inv-05

- Rule: Cross one distinct threshold together and hold connection.
- Purpose: Completes the escort action.
- Failure if removed: Separate crossings or a premature cut leave the outcome unclear.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Establish the protector and dependent role through visible contact before travel.
- Show the protector choose or confirm one route before escorting.
- Keep both identities and recurring landmarks traceable.
- Cross one distinct threshold together and hold connection.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source father and child
- the exact apartment and wardrobe
- the source embrace and escort choreography
- the exact glowing portal appearance
- the source family identity
