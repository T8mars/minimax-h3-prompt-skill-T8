# 不可能奢华姿态｜极远尺度钩子、连续推进与配饰细节阶梯 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one clearly adult original luxury subject in an impossible but readable pose against measurable vast scale. Use one continuous approach or coherent lens ladder to verify the same body, outfit and environment. Allow one restrained hand action to create a small controlled accent without disturbing the pose. Give face and one primary accessory separate readable material-proof intervals. Finish on a lower-body or product macro that retains the same light and environment rather than cutting to an unrelated packshot. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.
