# 符号布局单步改写｜空间揭示、手部决策与实体后果 Template

## Mechanism

Begin inside an original symbolic layout at object height so its markers read as spatial architecture before revealing a much larger entity or machine causally bound to that layout. Introduce one external hand or manipulator, keep the surrounding configuration stable, and change exactly one clearly identifiable marker. Do not add a second move. End with one restrained, observable state change in the bound full-scale entity or environment that proves the small intervention altered a larger system. The reusable mechanism is layout-as-world, scale reveal, single deliberate intervention and embodied consequence—not the source chess pieces, king, board, armored hand, crown, palace, fantasy design, exact move, camera route, color treatment, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Travel through an original symbolic layout at marker height before revealing its larger system.
- Purpose: Lets the audience experience the abstract arrangement as a physical world.
- Failure if removed: A top-down diagram alone cannot establish the later scale reversal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Bind the layout to one much larger entity, machine or environment in a single wide reveal.
- Purpose: Makes later symbolic intervention consequential beyond the tabletop.
- Failure if removed: Without a bound system, moving a marker remains decorative.

### inv-03

- Rule: Introduce one external hand or manipulator while surrounding markers remain stable.
- Purpose: Identifies a deliberate decision-maker and preserves comparison context.
- Failure if removed: Many simultaneous changes obscure agency and causality.

### inv-04

- Rule: Change exactly one clearly identifiable marker and show contact.
- Purpose: Creates one readable strategic cause instead of a montage of operations.
- Failure if removed: A hidden or multiple move cannot be mapped to the final result.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on one restrained state change in the bound system.
- Purpose: Confirms the larger consequence without replacing the idea with spectacle.
- Failure if removed: No response leaves the symbolic action unresolved; excessive action hides the causal link.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source black and white chess pieces, board squares, exact arrangement, move and checkmate implication
- the source seated king-like ruler, face, crown, armor, cloak, throne, hand and glowing-eye treatment
- the source palace-scale chess world, armies, architecture, snow or particles and fantasy iconography
- the source low chess traversal, central reveal, macro hand move, facial close-up, timing and cinematic rendering
- the source creator identity, LouieVerse naming, NANOCINEMA text, watermark, prompt-sharing claim and branding
