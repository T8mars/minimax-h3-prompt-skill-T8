# 微缩介入｜外部物只进入一次 Template

## Mechanism

Use one familiar inert object at the opening to calibrate a miniature world immediately. Let the small actors, tools, motion, shadows and atmosphere then sustain a coherent routine while the calibrator remains passive. Later, introduce one different external intervention tool exactly once, let it change one local state, and hold the intervention, affected object and internal reaction together so the story shifts from scale observation to outside control.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Calibrate scale early with one familiar inert standard object.
- Purpose: Makes scale an established fact rather than a delayed trick.
- Failure if removed: Without calibration the later intervention is mistaken for a scale reveal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Prove the known miniature world through coordinated routine and local physics.
- Purpose: Gives the internal system temporary autonomy.
- Failure if removed: A static model offers no relation to disrupt.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep the opening calibrator passive after its proof.
- Purpose: Separates measurement from agency.
- Failure if removed: An active calibrator collapses the two external roles.

### inv-04

- Rule: Introduce one different external intervention tool exactly once in one continuous action.
- Purpose: Creates a clear observer-to-controller transition.
- Failure if removed: Repeated tools or cuts turn the event into ordinary handling.

### inv-05

- Rule: Show one changed local state and internal reaction with both scales visible.
- Purpose: Proves intervention rather than proximity.
- Failure if removed: A hovering tool without consequence does not alter the relationship.

| sound_family | A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting state changes. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 静态标准物先校准尺度
- 微型日常先稳定建立
- 外部执行物与校准物不是同一物
- 外部物只介入一次且结果与反应同框

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- miniature barbecue on a kitchen table
- full-size human hand entering the scene
- foreground fork and wooden tabletop
- tiny men in colorful sweaters around a smoking grill

## Transfer Tests

1. A standard sewing needle calibrates a tiny alpine weather station on rough bark from the opening; later one insulated magnetic probe enters once and rotates a jammed vane while technicians react.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A paper clip establishes a miniature harbor's scale beside a shell groove; later one suction pipette enters once and lifts a stranded buoy into its channel.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
