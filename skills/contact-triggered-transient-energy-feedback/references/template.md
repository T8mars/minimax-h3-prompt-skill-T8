# 接触后放电｜真实格斗先成立、短促能量只做结果反馈 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult fighter, readable opponents and a stable combat geography before spectacle grows. Every decisive beat must follow the same causal order: an adult commits a visible physical trajectory, bodies or a prop make real contact, and only then a brief bounded energy, dust, hair, fabric or light reaction appears at the contact point. Keep the reaction shorter than the initiating action and preserve silhouettes throughout. Escalate through opponent count and reaction radius rather than pre-emptive projectiles. End by stopping all effects and holding the same adult in the established space. The reusable mechanism is physical action first, contact proof second, transient multimodal feedback third, controlled escalation and quiet reset—not the source people, gym, costumes, choreography, exact discharge design, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Show one clearly adult original fighter, opponents and a stable ground relation before the first effect.
- Purpose: Creates physical stakes and readable geography.
- Failure if removed: Without the baseline, later light could be mistaken for the cause of motion.

### inv-02

- Rule: Require a visible committed trajectory and real contact before every energy reaction.
- Purpose: Preserves action-to-effect causality.
- Failure if removed: An early flash turns the sequence into projectile magic and hides performance.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep every reaction bounded to the contact point and shorter than the initiating action.
- Purpose: Makes spectacle support rather than replace choreography.
- Failure if removed: Long full-frame blooms erase impact timing and body continuity.

### inv-04

- Rule: Escalate opponent count and reaction radius while keeping silhouettes and positions legible.
- Purpose: Raises intensity without abandoning the combat grammar.
- Failure if removed: Random camera and effect growth becomes visual noise.

### inv-05

- Rule: Stop all effects and hold the same adult in the established space after the final exchange.
- Purpose: Proves consequence and identity continuity.
- Failure if removed: An effect-only ending cannot show who remained or where.
