# 触觉攀登风险递增｜近身工具证据到峰顶尺度释放 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult climber as a small readable figure against a single overwhelming vertical objective. Alternate environmental scale views with tactile close evidence of at least three different load-bearing contacts such as tool bite, foot placement and rope tension. Introduce one recoverable contact failure that visibly lowers or rotates the adult, then show the arrest and a new valid placement in the same geography before ascent continues. Preserve at least three equipment and costume anchors throughout. Cross the final ridge in close view, then release into one stable wide that proves summit arrival and completed scale without celebration. The reusable mechanism is scale objective, tactile contact evidence, one recoverable failure, continuity-bearing equipment, ridge crossing and summit-scale release—not the source woman, face, clothing, ice axes, mountain, K2 claim, exact stunt, camera, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one adult climber as a small figure against one dominant vertical objective.
- Purpose: Makes height and direction measurable.
- Failure if removed: Close action without scale has no destination.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show at least three distinct load-bearing contacts in tactile close evidence.
- Purpose: Makes progress physical rather than decorative.
- Failure if removed: Generic arm motion cannot prove climbing.

### inv-03

- Rule: Include one recoverable contact failure, visible loss and explicit arrest in the same geography.
- Purpose: Creates risk while preserving causal continuity.
- Failure if removed: A cut from slip to safety erases the recovery mechanism.

### inv-04

- Rule: Preserve at least three equipment or costume anchors across every scale change.
- Purpose: Maintains adult identity and route continuity.
- Failure if removed: Changing gear makes the summit figure uncertain.

### inv-05

- Rule: Cross the ridge close, then end on one stable wide proving arrival without celebration.
- Purpose: Converts tactile effort into scale payoff.
- Failure if removed: A close victory pose substitutes emotion for spatial proof.
