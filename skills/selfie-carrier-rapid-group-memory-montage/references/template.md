# 群体随拍｜自拍锚点、快切活动到共同收束 Template

## Mechanism

Use one clearly adult original selfie carrier as the identity anchor across a rapid native-phone montage in one coherent gathering. Alternate short activity inserts with repeated returns to the carrier, and let the visible group grow in countable stages: setup, task, arrivals, peak collective reaction and complete shared gesture. Preserve recurring environmental anchors, ordinary jump cuts, slight shake, focus breathing and late recentering without losing the carrier. End on the largest readable group state and one completed communal action. The reusable mechanism is selfie anchor, activity proof, progressive group growth, imperfect phone grammar and terminal communal gesture—not the source woman, face, sports clothing, stadium, parking lot, car trunks, grills, burgers, drinks, team gear, friends, exact prompt, timing, creator identity or model label.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Keep one clearly adult original selfie carrier recognizable across every phase.
- Purpose: Provides identity continuity through rapid cuts.
- Failure if removed: Changing the carrier turns the sequence into an unrelated compilation.

### inv-02

- Rule: Stay within one coherent gathering and repeat at least two environmental anchors.
- Purpose: Makes jump cuts read as one event.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated locations destroy memory continuity.

### inv-03

- Rule: Alternate activity proof with returns to the carrier rather than showing only faces.
- Purpose: Answers what the group is doing and why it grows.
- Failure if removed: A face-only montage lacks causal event progression.

### inv-04

- Rule: Increase the readable group state through at least three stages while retaining natural imperfect phone behavior.
- Purpose: Builds social escalation without staged coverage.
- Failure if removed: A constant group or polished stabilization flattens the candid arc.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on the largest group state and one complete shared action.
- Purpose: Provides a countable terminal memory payoff.
- Failure if removed: Ending mid-pan or before the gesture leaves the event unresolved.
