# 感官封闭｜人群静止点到异地恢复 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one stable subject as a quiet center inside a visibly active environment. Let the subject perform one deliberate self-sealing gesture, keep external motion escalating while the subject becomes still, then use one full-frame perceptual wash to replace the environment. Restore the same identity and optical axis in a materially different location and require one visible resumption action that proves the new space persists.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one stable subject against independently moving surroundings before the trigger.
- Purpose: Creates a measurable calm-versus-flow baseline.
- Failure if removed: A quiet or empty opening provides no pressure to isolate from.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one deliberate bilateral or otherwise complete self-sealing gesture without changing location yet.
- Purpose: Makes the transition voluntary and countable.
- Failure if removed: An unexplained flash removes causal ownership.

### inv-03

- Rule: Continue environmental motion while the sealed subject reaches a visibly still state.
- Purpose: Builds separation before the decisive swap.
- Failure if removed: Stopping everything together erases the subject-environment contrast.

### inv-04

- Rule: Replace the whole environment only once through a bounded full-frame perceptual wash while preserving the subject axis.
- Purpose: Makes the swap decisive without a montage of portals.
- Failure if removed: Multiple partial swaps or a new camera axis break continuity.

### inv-05

- Rule: Restore the same identity in a materially different location and show one resumption action under the new physics.
- Purpose: Proves that the destination persists after the transition.
- Failure if removed: Ending in white light or on a static portrait leaves the spatial change unverified.
