# 见证释放｜封闭近景—外部尺度—双视图证明 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with a close witness-owned view inside a bounded enclosure while an external phenomenon is partially visible through a fixed opening. Use one visible directional action to motivate a camera release from the enclosure, then give the phenomenon an independent wide-scale proof. Finish with a simultaneous dual-view device that keeps witness response and environmental magnitude readable together; the dual proof may use an inset, reflection or monitor and must not copy the source's exact split layout.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one close witness inside a bounded enclosure and one partially visible external phenomenon through a fixed opening.
- Purpose: Creates an immediate scale question with a stable near-far relation.
- Failure if removed: An open wide beginning removes the witness-to-scale reveal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one visible directional action by the witness to transfer attention toward the external phenomenon.
- Purpose: Motivates the coming camera release.
- Failure if removed: An unmotivated cut outside separates reaction from what is being witnessed.

### inv-03

- Rule: Release the viewpoint from the enclosure through one readable physical boundary or carrier handoff.
- Purpose: Turns subjective evidence into independently inspectable space.
- Failure if removed: Teleporting to a wide shot loses the experiential transition.

### inv-04

- Rule: Give the phenomenon one unobstructed environment-scale view without the witness dominating it.
- Purpose: Proves magnitude beyond the close reaction frame.
- Failure if removed: Remaining in selfie scale never validates the phenomenon's spatial extent.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with a simultaneous dual proof of witness response and environmental phenomenon using a new composite geometry.
- Purpose: Keeps human-scale and environment-scale evidence available together.
- Failure if removed: Alternating them again weakens the final comparison and closure.
