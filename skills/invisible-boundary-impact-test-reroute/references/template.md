# 不可见边界验真｜碰撞、手测与改道离开 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult traveler, one simple route and a stable camera axis that makes the space ahead look fully passable. Let the adult approach continuously until exactly one unseen boundary contact produces a readable full-body stop or recoil; preserve the approach, contact and consequence without a cut across the causal moment. Show one brief self-check on the affected body area, then return to the exact contact location for one controlled palm test. Reveal the boundary only through localized physical evidence such as pressure, condensation, particles or ripples while keeping the broader surface visually transparent. End with the adult making a clear rerouting decision and leaving the blocked axis while the boundary remains spatially stable. The reusable mechanism is false passability, one impact, bodily evidence, deliberate retest, localized invisible-plane proof and route correction—not the source character, face, hair, costume, corridor, magic accessory, ripple design, exact gestures, composition, watermark, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult original traveler, a simple route and a stable axis that visibly predicts free passage.
- Purpose: Creates the expectation that the invisible boundary can later contradict.
- Failure if removed: If the route already looks blocked, impact reveals nothing.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use exactly one uninterrupted unseen contact and preserve approach, contact and bodily recoil.
- Purpose: Makes the hidden plane physically credible before visual effects explain it.
- Failure if removed: Cutting across contact or repeating impacts weakens the causal proof.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show one brief self-check that identifies the affected body area before any deliberate test.
- Purpose: Separates accidental impact from later investigation.
- Failure if removed: Immediate magic visualization would skip human diagnosis.

### inv-04

- Rule: Return to the same location for one controlled palm test and localize the boundary response around contact.
- Purpose: Turns inference into repeatable spatial evidence without making the whole wall opaque.
- Failure if removed: A global effect cannot prove where or how the surface was touched.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on a clear rerouting decision while the confirmed boundary stays fixed in the original path.
- Purpose: Converts discovery into behavioral consequence and closes the route logic.
- Failure if removed: Walking through or forgetting the plane erases the evidence chain.
