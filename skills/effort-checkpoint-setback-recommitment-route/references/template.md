# 训练复投｜计时检查触发受挫后重新上路 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with one clearly adult trainee sustaining a directional physical task long enough to prove effort. Move into a second, mechanically different training action that serves the same objective. Introduce one visible instrument or countable checkpoint only after the effort baseline; let its result trigger a brief setback with observable physiological residue. Allow one bounded recovery action, then require the trainee to re-enter the same route or task direction before any emotional resolution. End on hydration, breath control or equipment reset plus a steady recommitment gaze—never an instant skill upgrade or victory. The reusable mechanism is effort, measurement, setback, bounded recovery, route re-entry and future commitment—not the source magical-girl design, hair, costume, road, park, watch, observers, tears, bottle, exact anime style, camera, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Prove sustained effort in one directional physical task before showing a result.
- Purpose: Makes the checkpoint emotionally earned.
- Failure if removed: Immediate measurement provides no evidence of investment.

### inv-02

- Rule: Add a second mechanically different exercise that serves the same objective.
- Purpose: Shows training breadth without changing goals.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated activity fragments the attempt.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one visible instrument or countable checkpoint to trigger the setback.
- Purpose: Externalizes why the trainee's interpretation changes.
- Failure if removed: Unmotivated sadness makes the arc arbitrary.

### inv-04

- Rule: Keep brief physiological residue through one bounded recovery action, then visibly re-enter the same route or task.
- Purpose: Turns recovery into agency without erasing difficulty.
- Failure if removed: Instant neutrality or a cut to success skips the recommitment choice.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with a practical reset and steady commitment, not an immediate victory or power upgrade.
- Purpose: Keeps the payoff honest and reusable for persistence stories.
- Failure if removed: A sudden triumph contradicts the measured setback.

## Transfer Tests

1. An adult wildfire-rescue trainee drags a weighted hose down one marked lane, completes a different stair carry, checks a lap timer, reacts to a missed threshold, cools wrists once, re-enters the same lane and ends tightening gloves at sunset with a steady gaze.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A stop-motion clay courier pushes a cart along a scored paper route, practices two ramp transfers, reads a mechanical counter, slumps when one token remains, winds the cart spring once, resumes the same groove and ends beside a lantern resetting the route marker.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
