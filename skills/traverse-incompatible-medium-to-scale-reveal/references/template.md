# 异质航行｜维修出发到巨尺度揭示 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one carrier, its crew and a visible readiness task before departure. Move that same carrier through an environment whose material is incompatible with its familiar travel grammar, use close and wide views to prove continuous control and increasing route scale, then end with a late giant-scale environmental, living or working-form reveal that reframes how small the journey has been. The source analysis need not identify that form's purpose; a transfer may assign an original function as instance-specific fiction.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one carrier, its crew and a visible readiness task before departure.
- Purpose: Makes later performance earned and inspectable.
- Failure if removed: An unexplained moving carrier lacks a causal start.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show an unambiguous engine or control engagement followed by departure.
- Purpose: Closes preparation and opens the journey.
- Failure if removed: A cut from repair to distant travel loses the readiness handoff.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep one carrier and one forward route continuous across an incompatible material environment.
- Purpose: Turns the setting contrast into sustained travel evidence.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated scenic inserts cannot prove a journey.

### inv-04

- Rule: Alternate local control proof with wider speed-and-scale proof.
- Purpose: Separates crew response from route magnitude.
- Failure if removed: Only close shots hide route scale; only wides hide control.

### inv-05

- Rule: Use a late giant-scale environmental, living or working-form reveal to reframe the journey's scale.
- Purpose: Adds a final scale context after local and wide travel proof.
- Failure if removed: Ending on another travel shot adds no new context; any assigned function must belong to the new transfer rather than being claimed as source evidence.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is readable without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| carrier_or_token | One visible material, prop or symbolic anchor that carries the causal mechanism. |
| state_change | A sequence of visible, causally ordered changes rather than decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each change without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final state that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of visible-action sounds; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- white-haired desert mechanic and large-eyed animal companion
- sand-sailing craft and ocean-like dune route
- source repair tools, canyon chase and whale-shaped landform
- the exact source desert palette, character design and travel storyboard
