# 威胁接力反转｜救援改写层级并重启追逐 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult vulnerable protagonist, one first-order threat and a visible rescue direction inside a stable environment. Let a materially larger second-order threat enter the same geography and directly remove the first threat, but immediately inherit pursuit of the protagonist. Show the protagonist carrying a visible injury or exhaustion state toward at least two clearly adult rescuers; their first intervention creates only a temporary suppression and exposes one usable tool. The protagonist acquires that tool and joins the rescuers, then the second-order threat visibly resumes movement or re-enters before the end. The reusable mechanism is threat substitution, inherited pursuit, physically continuous escape, provisional rescue, tool acquisition and renewed collective defense—not the source survivor, creatures, forest, flame, weapons, uniforms, choreography, gore, watermark, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult vulnerable protagonist, a first-order threat and a visible rescue direction in one geography.
- Purpose: Gives later threat substitution and escape a measurable baseline.
- Failure if removed: Without a rescue direction, each creature encounter reads as an unrelated insert.

### inv-02

- Rule: Let a larger second-order threat directly remove the first threat and immediately inherit pursuit of the same protagonist.
- Purpose: Turns apparent rescue into an escalation rather than a reset.
- Failure if removed: If the larger threat ignores the protagonist, the danger chain ends instead of transferring.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry one visible injury or exhaustion state through the protagonist's movement toward at least two adult rescuers.
- Purpose: Preserves the cost of the first conflict and makes rescue urgency legible.
- Failure if removed: A fully reset sprint erases causal continuity.

### inv-04

- Rule: Make the rescuers' first intervention only temporarily suppress the larger threat while exposing one usable tool.
- Purpose: Creates a short reversal that enables the protagonist to change role.
- Failure if removed: An instant permanent defeat leaves no reason for tool acquisition or cooperation.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let the protagonist acquire the tool and join collective defense before the larger threat resumes movement or re-enters.
- Purpose: Pays off the rescue as capability transfer while retaining threat escalation.
- Failure if removed: Ending on the collapsed threat falsely resolves the core mechanism.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source adult survivor, face, body, clothing, injuries, expressions and exact performance
- the source spider-like creature, larger forest monster, anatomy, attacks, mouth, limbs and gore
- the source bioluminescent jungle, cyan plants, mud, roots, flame and exact lighting
- the source soldiers, rifles, uniforms, shooting, crawling, combat choreography and camera path
- the source creator identity, Topview challenge mark, Seedance 2.5 claim, watermark and 30-second timing
