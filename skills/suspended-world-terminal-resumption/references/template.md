# 世界悬停｜持续靠近到末端复流 Template

## Mechanism

Establish a motion-rich environment in a visibly suspended state, then let one embodied observer move continuously through it while recurring objects prove the world remains locked. Introduce one local inspection alignment without requiring contact, sustain the frozen comparison, and reserve one terminal global resumption in which many independent elements and the camera regain motion together.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Open on a complex environment whose normally moving elements are visibly suspended.
- Purpose: Creates an unmistakable stopped-world baseline.
- Failure if removed: A quiet empty environment cannot prove suspension.

### inv-02

- Rule: Move one embodied observer continuously while at least three environmental landmarks remain frozen.
- Purpose: Separates observer motion from world time.
- Failure if removed: Cuts or changing landmarks make the state ambiguous.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one local alignment or inspection focus, but do not require unproven physical contact.
- Purpose: Concentrates attention without inventing causality.
- Failure if removed: A forced touch claim exceeds the visible mechanism.

### inv-04

- Rule: Sustain the suspended comparison long enough to show that many independent elements share the same state.
- Purpose: Builds contrast for the release.
- Failure if removed: A brief freeze frame reads as an editing trick.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resume the world only once at the end, with distributed object motion and camera-speed change occurring together.
- Purpose: Provides a global terminal payoff.
- Failure if removed: Multiple resumptions dilute the state transition.

## Transfer Tests

1. Inside an original orbital assembly bay, a helmet-camera technician walks among halted robot arms, floating bolts, paused coolant beads and a stopped cargo ring. A gloved probe aligns with one calibration prism without touching it; only at the end do all machines, beads and the camera corridor resume together.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. In stop-motion paper printing, one tiny inspector walks across a press while rollers, paper scraps, ink drops and gear teeth remain suspended. A magnifier aligns with a single registration dot, the hold persists, then the entire press advances exactly once and pulls the camera into the finished sheet.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
