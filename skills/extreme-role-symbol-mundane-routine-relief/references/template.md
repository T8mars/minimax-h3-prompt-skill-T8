# 极端职业符号落入日常｜身份揭示到休假松弛 Template

## Mechanism

Establish a densely legible mundane institution and its normal group rhythm before introducing one original adult visitor whose silhouette, equipment or uniform communicates an extreme high-stakes profession. Carry one personal travel object through the entrance and let background adults react first, creating a credible threat or disruption expectation. Reveal the visitor's identity at the institutional threshold, then immediately replace the expected dangerous action with an ordinary administrative object or task. Show the same visitor assimilated into a repetitive everyday routine, and finish with one readable status token plus a relaxed physical action that proves temporary relief. The reusable mechanism is iconic role contrast, social expectation, identity reveal, mundane substitution, routine assimilation and explicit off-duty payoff—not the source Grim Reaper, skull, scythe associations, black robe, suitcase stickers, office design, employees, paperwork arrangement, cup, exact sign wording, composition, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish the ordinary institution and its group rhythm before the extreme-role visitor enters.
- Purpose: Creates a clear baseline that the silhouette can disrupt.
- Failure if removed: Without a mundane baseline, the visitor is only character design.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give the visitor one persistent travel object and a profession-readable silhouette while surrounding adults react first.
- Purpose: Builds social expectation without exposition.
- Failure if removed: If nobody notices and no object persists, arrival has little narrative pressure.

### inv-03

- Rule: Pair one identity reveal with an immediate substitution of an ordinary administrative object for the expected high-stakes action.
- Purpose: Turns threat into the central role-inversion joke.
- Failure if removed: A reveal without substitution preserves menace instead of changing meaning.

### inv-04

- Rule: Show the same visitor absorbed into one repetitive everyday routine with institutional anchors intact.
- Purpose: Proves assimilation rather than a one-shot costume gag.
- Failure if removed: Cutting to an unrelated leisure scene breaks the institution-to-relief chain.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on one readable off-duty status token and one relaxed action in the same routine space.
- Purpose: Makes the relief state explicit and visually verifiable.
- Failure if removed: A relaxed pose alone may not explain why the extreme role has paused.
