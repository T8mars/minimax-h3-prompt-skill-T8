# 尺度球局｜共享球体串联反差挑战 Template

## Mechanism

A scale-mismatch challenge stays coherent when one shared token remains visible across control, obstruction, route change and an attempt toward a fixed target. Court or arena markings must preserve geography, and the ending may show reaction or suspense without claiming a result that is not visibly confirmed.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly different scales, one shared token and one fixed target in the same arena.
- Purpose: Makes the challenge measurable.
- Failure if removed: Hiding any one element reduces the scene to unrelated spectacle.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use repeated token control to prove who currently owns the action.
- Purpose: Maintains continuity through scale contrast.
- Failure if removed: A disappearing token breaks the causal chain.

### inv-03

- Rule: Let the larger body obstruct space while the smaller body changes route rather than matching size.
- Purpose: Keeps scale functionally relevant.
- Failure if removed: Equalized movement makes the size contrast cosmetic.

### inv-04

- Rule: Show one readable token trajectory toward the fixed target.
- Purpose: Converts maneuvering into a clear attempt.
- Failure if removed: A cut directly to reaction hides the challenge's decisive action.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on a visible shared reaction state and only claim success if the target result is directly shown.
- Purpose: Preserves evidence fidelity and suspense.
- Failure if removed: An unsupported score claim invents the payoff.

## Transfer Tests

1. On a tabletop magnetic arena, a coin-sized delivery robot controls one glowing bead while a giant inflatable gatekeeper sweeps broad sectors. The robot changes lanes and launches the bead toward a suspended copper ring; the ending holds both reactions and the ring, without declaring success unless the bead visibly passes through.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. In a circular zero-gravity greenhouse, a small maintenance pod guides one water sphere around a huge slow robotic leaf. Floor rings and a fixed seed funnel preserve geography; the pod redirects the sphere toward the funnel and the film holds the unresolved arrival moment.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
