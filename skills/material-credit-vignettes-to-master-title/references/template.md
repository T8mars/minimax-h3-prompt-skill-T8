# 材质片头｜多件物证承载职员表到总标题 Template

## Mechanism

Build a short title sequence from at least three materially different object vignettes. Give each vignette one concise original credit and one material-specific motion that frames rather than obscures the text. Repeat one color, geometric or token motif across the vignettes, then return to an established material family and let the recurring motif bridge into one large master title. Hold the final title without further transformation. The reusable mechanism is material-specific credit relay, recurring motif, gathered transition and master-title lockup—not the source ancient setting, names, relief map, red channel, green stones, mirror vessel, fire figure, pyramid forms, green sphere, exact title, typeface, scene order, creator identity or MiniMax H3 branding.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Use at least three materially distinct object vignettes, each with one short readable original credit.
- Purpose: Creates a credit relay rather than a single decorative title.
- Failure if removed: Repeating one surface makes the middle states interchangeable.

### inv-02

- Rule: Bind each credit to one visible material-specific motion that never covers the text.
- Purpose: Makes typography part of the physical scene while preserving readability.
- Failure if removed: Generic crossfades or occluded copy remove the tactile proof.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry one color, shape or token motif through every vignette.
- Purpose: Provides continuity across incompatible materials.
- Failure if removed: No recurring motif turns the sequence into unrelated product shots.

### inv-04

- Rule: Return to one earlier material family before the master title and use the motif as the bridge.
- Purpose: Gathers prior evidence into a motivated finale.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated final background makes the title feel appended.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve one large master title and hold it fully readable with no further event.
- Purpose: Provides the terminal information state.
- Failure if removed: Continuing transformations prevent title retention.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source names, credits, title wording, typeface and exact typographic hierarchy
- the source ancient relief map, red and turquoise channels, pyramid-like blocks and terrain layout
- the source green stone ring, black textile, mirror vessel, clay pot, flame and miniature figure
- the source green sphere, title sculpture, exact material order, camera moves and lighting cues
- the source creator identity, MiniMax H3 claim, platform overlay, watermark and branding
