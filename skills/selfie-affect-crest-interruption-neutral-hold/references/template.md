# 自拍情绪峰值回落｜亲近笑意、中断与中性停留 Template

## Mechanism

Lock one original clearly adult subject in a stable arm-length smartphone close frame, one low-light environment and one direct lens relationship. Begin from a readable affiliative smile and increase positive affect through at least two continuous microexpression stages until a held crest is clear in cheeks, eyes and mouth. Introduce one restrained offscreen directional cue without showing a new person or cutting away; let the same adult open the eyes, redirect attention and release the smile in a continuous transition. Use one breath or small posture adjustment to settle into a neutral or mildly concerned terminal state, then hold long enough to distinguish it from a blink. The reusable mechanism is fixed selfie identity, graded affect rise, measurable crest, offscreen interruption, continuous release and neutral emotional residue—not the source face, blue hair, jacket, hood, night palette, exact expressions, smartphone framing, creator identity, prompt wording or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original subject, arm-length selfie geometry, low-light environment and direct lens relationship before affect changes.
- Purpose: Makes later facial states comparable under one stable identity and camera distance.
- Failure if removed: Reframing or identity drift can mimic an emotional change.

### inv-02

- Rule: Raise positive affect through at least two continuous microexpression stages.
- Purpose: Builds a readable emotional curve rather than toggling between presets.
- Failure if removed: A single jump to laughter removes progression.

### inv-03

- Rule: Hold one measurable crest in cheeks, eyes and mouth before interruption.
- Purpose: Creates a clear peak state against which release can be judged.
- Failure if removed: Without a held crest, the later neutral state lacks contrast.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use one restrained offscreen directional cue to cause eye opening, attention shift and smile release without adding a visible person.
- Purpose: Supplies narrative causality while preserving the one-face frame.
- Failure if removed: An unexplained cut or visible second character changes the mechanism.

### inv-05

- Rule: Settle through breath or small posture regulation into a sustained neutral or mildly concerned terminal hold.
- Purpose: Proves emotional residue rather than a transient blink.
- Failure if removed: Ending during the transition leaves the final state ambiguous.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source adult face, short bright-blue curls, eye shape, nose jewelry and exact expressions
- the source plum hooded quilted jacket, orange lining, constellation stitching and black inner collar
- the source dim red-green night lighting, dark background and exact vertical phone framing
- the source exact smile progression, closed-eye laughter, wide-eye interruption, neutral settling and microtiming
- the source creator identity, Seedance 2.5 wording, truncated prompt text and 15-second timing
