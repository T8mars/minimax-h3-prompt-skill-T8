# 被拍到的一天｜观察者瑕疵伴随路线，人物最终发现镜头 Template

## Mechanism

Follow one original clearly adult subject through at least four ordinary route chapters with stable clothing, carried-object or gait anchors. Maintain deliberate but bounded observer imperfections—slightly late pans, short foreground occlusions, modest focus recovery and breathing-scale sway—while always returning to the subject and route. The subject must avoid direct camera acknowledgment until the final chapter. End with one unmistakable eye-line change and short approach that reveals awareness of the recorder. The reusable mechanism is ordinary route continuity, controlled observer imperfection, delayed acknowledgment and camera-discovery closure—not the source man, apartment, market, fruit, cat, rooftop, exact framing, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original subject with at least three clothing, object or gait anchors across four ordinary chapters.
- Purpose: Maintains identity through everyday cuts.
- Failure if removed: Unanchored chapter changes suggest different people or days.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use only bounded observer imperfections and always recover the subject and route.
- Purpose: Creates authentic home-video presence without losing readability.
- Failure if removed: Continuous random shake or focus loss becomes unusable footage.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry one object, direction or completed action from each chapter into the next.
- Purpose: Links small events into a day.
- Failure if removed: Independent activities form a loose montage.

### inv-04

- Rule: Do not let the subject directly acknowledge the camera before the final chapter.
- Purpose: Preserves the observer relationship and delayed reveal.
- Failure if removed: Early eye contact removes the ending's meaning.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with one clear eye-line change and short approach toward the lens.
- Purpose: Makes camera discovery observable.
- Failure if removed: A generic wave or cut cannot prove the subject noticed recording.
