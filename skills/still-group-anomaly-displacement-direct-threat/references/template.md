# 静态群像异常升级｜表情异变、空间击穿与贴脸威胁 Template

## Mechanism

Open on one stable social group of clearly adult original characters in a readable room and identify one focal adult through at least three persistent visual anchors. Introduce one localized facial anomaly without changing identity, then escalate exactly once into a traceable displacement of the group, camera or room state. Hold the same environment briefly without the focal adult so the audience reads aftermath and anticipates a return. Reintroduce the same altered adult from an established depth axis and let proximity, not extra effects, drive the final escalation until the face occupies the frame. The reusable mechanism is ordinary group baseline, identity-preserving facial anomaly, one spatial rupture, empty-space consequence gap and same-identity direct approach—not the source people, faces, couch, room, black eyes, hair, exact expressions, motion, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish a calm adult group, one focal identity and stable room geometry before any anomaly.
- Purpose: Creates a measurable safety baseline.
- Failure if removed: Starting in chaos removes the escalation contrast.

### inv-02

- Rule: Change one localized feature while preserving at least three identity anchors.
- Purpose: Makes the anomaly belong to the same person.
- Failure if removed: A redesigned face reads as a new subject or continuity error.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one spatially traceable group or camera displacement as the major rupture.
- Purpose: Converts private unease into physical consequence.
- Failure if removed: Repeated random shakes become noise rather than progression.

### inv-04

- Rule: Hold the established environment briefly while withholding the focal adult.
- Purpose: Creates aftermath evidence and a directional question.
- Failure if removed: Immediate reappearance gives no consequence gap.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return the same altered adult from depth and end on increasing proximity rather than a new effect.
- Purpose: Turns preserved identity into the final threat.
- Failure if removed: A different attacker or unrelated jump cut breaks the causal chain.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source adult group, woman, faces, hair, clothing, couch and exact expressions
- the source domestic room, furniture, walls, floor, openings and exact staging
- the source solid black eyes, possession appearance and hair transformation
- the source camera displacement, group motion, low angles, approach and terminal close-up
- the source creator identity, Seedance 2.5 wording and 20-second timing
