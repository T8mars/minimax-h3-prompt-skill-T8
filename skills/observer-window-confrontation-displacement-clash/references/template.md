# 见证窗不切换｜对峙、越界位移与能量碰撞留在同一场 Template

## Mechanism

Choose one original observer boundary such as a window, booth or balcony and use it to establish two clearly adult participants, a collision lane and at least one environmental consequence surface. Keep that boundary recoverable through the sequence. After a readable confrontation baseline, cause one participant to cross the gap and displace the other into the consequence surface; then return both participants to the same arena with the damage retained. Build two opposed charge states across the original axis and let the final collision expand into or obscure the observer boundary. The reusable mechanism is bounded witness geography, displacement-to-damage causality, arena return and opposed collision—not the source school, students, copyrighted characters, hairstyles, costumes, crowd, powers, wall, camera position, style, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one observer boundary, two adult participants, the collision lane and a consequence surface before escalation.
- Purpose: Gives every impossible action a measurable shared arena.
- Failure if removed: Without a baseline, displacement and scale cannot be verified.

### inv-02

- Rule: Carry the first gap closure into one visible environmental damage result.
- Purpose: Makes the attack legible through a destination and residue.
- Failure if removed: A fast blur without a consequence reads as a missing shot.

### inv-03

- Rule: Return both participants to the original arena while retaining the damage state.
- Purpose: Preserves event continuity and cumulative consequence.
- Failure if removed: Resetting the wall or changing location breaks the causal chain.

### inv-04

- Rule: Build two distinct opposed charge states across the established collision axis.
- Purpose: Prepares a readable terminal interaction rather than arbitrary effects.
- Failure if removed: Unoriented energy makes the final impact spatially ambiguous.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let the final collision transform or obscure the same observer boundary.
- Purpose: Closes participant action, environment and witness perspective together.
- Failure if removed: A detached explosion insert abandons the promised viewpoint.
