# 实拍印刷层接力｜人物、商品细节与字卡共同推进 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult campaign subject with at least three stable identity and wardrobe anchors in a live-action frame. Introduce a coherent print-material system—paper grain, torn masks, ink fields and concise original text—and require every new layer to be handed off by a visible subject action, garment detail or registered spatial edge. Alternate full-body proof, at least two material-detail macros, one environment-based performance and at least two substantially different poster layouts while preserving the same subject and garment family. Keep text short, readable and causally tied to the evidence beside it. End by gathering the previously proven subject, details and message hierarchy into one held campaign sheet. The reusable mechanism is live-subject-to-print registration, evidence-linked typography, material-detail relay, layout variation and consolidated poster closure—not the source adult, face, hair, garments, motorcycle, roadside setting, national imagery, decades, slogans, brands, palette, layouts, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult original subject with at least three stable identity and wardrobe anchors before the first print conversion.
- Purpose: Gives every later cutout, portrait and lineup a verifiable human and garment baseline.
- Failure if removed: Without a stable opening identity, poster variations can conceal character and wardrobe drift.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one coherent print-material system and let each new layer enter through a visible action, detail or registered spatial edge.
- Purpose: Turns collage transitions into causal handoffs rather than decorative overlays.
- Failure if removed: Arbitrary paper wipes can cover discontinuity without advancing the campaign.

### inv-03

- Rule: Alternate full-body proof with at least two distinct garment or object detail macros.
- Purpose: Connects campaign attitude to concrete material evidence.
- Failure if removed: A sequence of portraits and slogans alone does not prove the advertised design.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use at least two substantially different poster layouts while preserving subject, wardrobe and message hierarchy.
- Purpose: Demonstrates that the identity system survives editorial variation.
- Failure if removed: Repeating one composition with new words does not test reusable layout behavior.

### inv-05

- Rule: Gather previously proven subject, details and concise messages into one final held campaign sheet.
- Purpose: Creates a readable terminal state instead of ending mid-transition.
- Failure if removed: A final unrelated portrait leaves the evidence and message modules unresolved.
