# 规则闯关｜吸收能力后从平面赛道升维 Template

## Mechanism

Introduce one visually distinct outsider into a rule-bound flat route and establish one stable travel direction. Let the outsider first demonstrate the route's base grammar through movement, avoidance or collection, then acquire exactly one visible capability through physical contact with a clearly presented trigger. Use that capability to enter a genuinely different travel mode while preserving identity and destination. Near the finish, extrude the already-established route into a deeper spatial regime without resetting direction or objective, and hold the outsider at a readable goal so both rule learning and dimensional escalation can be verified.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one outsider, one flat rule-bound route and one stable destination direction before any upgrade.
- Purpose: Creates a baseline for both rule learning and later spatial escalation.
- Failure if removed: Starting after the upgrade removes proof that the subject learned or changed.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show the outsider successfully using at least two different base route rules before capability acquisition.
- Purpose: Proves participation in the world rather than decorative compositing.
- Failure if removed: An immediate capability change makes the earlier route irrelevant.

### inv-03

- Rule: Bind exactly one capability change to visible contact with a presented trigger.
- Purpose: Makes the upgrade causal and auditable.
- Failure if removed: An unexplained morph reads as a disconnected effect.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use the acquired capability to enter a different movement mode while keeping identity and destination continuous.
- Purpose: Turns the upgrade into functional progression.
- Failure if removed: A cosmetic change without new movement does not escalate the route.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return from the upgraded movement to one readable final approach, then extrude the established route near the finish and end at one goal without resetting direction.
- Purpose: Provides a visible handoff into the world-scale payoff while preserving route continuity.
- Failure if removed: A cut from the upgraded movement to an unrelated 3D finish would hide the route handoff.

It waits for a scanning bar to clear and rotates its mirrored face to redirect a locked light gate, proving two different local rules. Contact with one violet phase seal turns the token translucent, letting it pass through opaque index dividers without leaving the route. It returns to the illuminated baseline; near the destination the map rises into a three-dimensional archive atrium and the same token enters a white return slot.

It resurfaces on the same route; the flat plan then lifts into terraced greenhouse beds and the pod enters a single watering cradle.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
