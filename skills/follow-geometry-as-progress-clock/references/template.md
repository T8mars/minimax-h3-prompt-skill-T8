# 连续跟随｜步态与弯曲地标共同计时 Template

## Mechanism

Use one uninterrupted rear follow in a constrained curved passage. Keep the subject's gait and screen position stable while repeated fixed lights, rail joints and wall ribs pass as a countable progress clock; let the changing tunnel curvature slowly shift the vanishing point, then hold a visibly farther position without inventing a threshold, encounter or route change.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Begin with one stable rear-follow relationship inside a constrained passage.
- Purpose: Makes later progress measurable against the body.
- Failure if removed: Changing follow distance hides the route clock.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use repeated fixed landmarks as countable progress evidence.
- Purpose: Proves travel without cuts.
- Failure if removed: Featureless walls make movement ambiguous.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep gait, travel axis and body scale stable through the middle.
- Purpose: Separates environmental geometry change from performance change.
- Failure if removed: Speed ramps or body jumps fake progress.

### inv-04

- Rule: Let passage curvature shift the vanishing point gradually.
- Purpose: Adds visible geometric evolution without a new event.
- Failure if removed: A sudden turn creates a different mechanism.

### inv-05

- Rule: End at a materially farther position on the same route.
- Purpose: Closes the progress study honestly.
- Failure if removed: Inventing a threshold or discovery exceeds the source.
