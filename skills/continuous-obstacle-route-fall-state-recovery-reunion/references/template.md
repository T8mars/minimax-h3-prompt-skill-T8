# 连续闯关赴约｜湿身残留、障碍升级与终点重逢 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult participant, one continuous destination direction and one shared obstacle environment before the first crossing. Chain at least three materially different obstacle classes so each requires a different body strategy, and preserve camera, wardrobe and environmental continuity between them. Include one recoverable fall or immersion that leaves an observable state such as wet clothing, mud or dust; show the adult recovering inside the route and retain that state through all later obstacles. End only after an environmental response marks arrival and a waiting adult or concrete delivery target resolves the promised destination. The reusable mechanism is continuous route, locomotion-class escalation, failure-state persistence, in-route recovery and terminal reunion—not the source woman, man, faces, costumes, water garden, lotus pads, log, ropes, stones, birds, romance, mythology, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one adult participant, one destination direction and a shared obstacle environment before the first crossing.
- Purpose: Turns separate stunts into one coherent route with a measurable goal.
- Failure if removed: Without route geography, the sequence reads as unrelated obstacle inserts.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use at least three material obstacle classes that require different body strategies.
- Purpose: Creates genuine escalation through motion change rather than visual decoration.
- Failure if removed: Repeating the same jump cannot prove adaptive progress.

### inv-03

- Rule: Include one recoverable fall or immersion and preserve its visible state afterward.
- Purpose: Gives failure a cumulative physical consequence.
- Failure if removed: Instantly dry or clean continuity erases the route history.

### inv-04

- Rule: Show recovery inside the route and continue without resetting location or identity.
- Purpose: Keeps the failure causally connected to later effort.
- Failure if removed: A recovery cut to a new scene breaks the one-route promise.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve the destination through an environmental arrival cue and a waiting adult or delivery target.
- Purpose: Provides both spatial and narrative proof of completion.
- Failure if removed: Ending on the last obstacle leaves the motive unresolved.
