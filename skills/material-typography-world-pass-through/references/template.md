# 材质字构穿行｜同一成年人物跨九类物质语义空间 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one clearly adult original subject and a fixed dark spatial axis before material typography appears. Assign each module one short original word, one material family and one physical behavior. Keep the subject identity and travel direction continuous while material physics change. Compress prior modules through a bounded aperture, ribbon or paper relay rather than random cuts. Converge all material channels into one stable terminal portrait and readable original title. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.
