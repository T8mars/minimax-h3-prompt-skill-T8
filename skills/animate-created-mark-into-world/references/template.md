# 画中物成真｜小光点变奇观 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with a small mark being intentionally created on a bounded physical surface. Let it acquire autonomy before crossing the surface edge, preserve one continuous trajectory into the live environment, then multiply or scale until its emitted property changes the surrounding world. Creation, autonomy, crossing, multiplication and environmental consequence must occur in that order; medium, creature, setting, emission, scale and emotion are variable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Show the mark being completed on a readable physical surface.
- Purpose: Establishes authored origin.
- Failure if removed: A pre-existing magical object loses the creation-to-life premise.

### inv-02

- Rule: Prove autonomy while the mark is still near its source boundary.
- Purpose: Separates life from a hard cut or projection.
- Failure if removed: Crossing before lift-off makes the transition ambiguous.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one continuous path to cross from authored surface into shared space.
- Purpose: Binds the two ontological layers.
- Failure if removed: Teleportation removes the legible boundary event.

### inv-04

- Rule: Multiply or enlarge only after the first entity has crossed.
- Purpose: Creates a clear scale ladder.
- Failure if removed: Early swarm imagery skips the intimate proof.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let the scaled phenomenon visibly alter environmental light, motion or material.
- Purpose: Supplies a physical consequence.
- Failure if removed: Particles without environmental response remain decorative VFX.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original subject whose identity and silhouette remain stable. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| escalation_variable | One readable variable that changes monotonically or in countable stages. |
| performance_rule | One restrained performance rule that makes the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | A coherent information order that does not copy the source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | One clean physical or graphic consequence that closes the causal chain. |
| sound_family | A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting each state change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 起点明确是被创作的标记
- 先出现自主动作
- 必须跨过真实边界
- 扩张后改变环境而非只放大

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- yellow-pencil firefly drawn in a rain-covered sketchbook
- warm golden insect swarm in a gloomy urban alley
- source hand, page angle, street geography and pedestrian arrangement
- MiniMax or Hailuo watermark placement

## Transfer Tests

1. A costume maker stitches one silver crane outline into black velvet; it folds free, crosses a stage curtain seam, then unspools into reflective paper forms that unfold a dormant mirror mobile and redirect one spotlight into a geometric seat pattern.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A child paints a tiny red leaf on a fogged train window; the leaf peels into the carriage air and enlarges into a paper canopy whose shadow pattern changes every cold reflection.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
