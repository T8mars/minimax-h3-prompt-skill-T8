# 连续载具升级｜滑行、冲浪、生物接力到高空释放 Template

## Mechanism

Keep one original clearly adult protagonist moving continuously along one established direction. Use at least four locomotion states whose carrier, contact surface or scale changes, but require every change to occur at a visible physical threshold and inherit the previous velocity, body posture or wake. Escalate monotonically from ground contact to water surface, larger living or mechanical support, and finally increased height. Never reset the adult identity or movement axis between states. End with a brief elevated release that still preserves the same silhouette. The reusable mechanism is continuous motion inheritance, threshold-based carrier handoff, monotonic scale-and-height escalation and airborne payoff—not the source woman, island styling, skateboard, surfboard, animals, ocean, choreography, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original identity and one forward movement direction before the first handoff.
- Purpose: Creates continuity across carrier changes.
- Failure if removed: Changing identity or direction makes separate clips rather than one route.

### inv-02

- Rule: Trigger every carrier change at a visible physical threshold such as an edge, wake, contact or launch.
- Purpose: Gives each transition a causal bridge.
- Failure if removed: Open morphs or arbitrary cuts break motion inheritance.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry at least one velocity, posture or wake cue through every transition.
- Purpose: Proves that movement continues rather than restarts.
- Failure if removed: A stationary incoming carrier resets momentum.

### inv-04

- Rule: Escalate surface, carrier scale or height monotonically across at least four states.
- Purpose: Builds a readable progression.
- Failure if removed: Randomly shrinking or returning to earlier states weakens the payoff.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with the same adult silhouette released at the highest scale or altitude.
- Purpose: Completes the ladder while preserving identity.
- Failure if removed: An anonymous landscape or different rider loses the route.
