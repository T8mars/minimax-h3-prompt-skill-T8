# 速度对蛮力的非对称证明｜重击承诺、连续闪避与环境撞击 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult agile protagonist and one original heavy opponent on a stable ground axis with a visible environmental impact surface behind them. Make every heavy attack fully commit to a readable trajectory before the agile adult evades; preserve the opponent's mass through slower turns, longer recovery and environmental displacement. Let the agile adult win position through footwork, vaulting or redirected momentum rather than matching force. Include one partial contact or forced slide that the adult converts into recovery. End when the maximum committed attack misses the adult and permanently damages the previously established impact surface, then reveal the same adult outside the attack line. The reusable mechanism is asymmetric body design, committed force trajectory, speed-based evasion, one risk contact, environmental power proof and off-axis survival—not the source woman, face, black suit, armored giant, warehouse, crates, punches, wall break, choreography, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one adult agile body, one heavy opponent, one ground axis and one impact surface.
- Purpose: Makes both abilities and later consequences measurable.
- Failure if removed: Without anchors the duel becomes generic motion.

### inv-02

- Rule: Let every heavy attack commit to a visible trajectory before evasion.
- Purpose: Allows speed to be proven against a real risk.
- Failure if removed: Dodging before commitment looks preplanned rather than fast.

### inv-03

- Rule: Preserve mass through slower turns, recovery time and environmental displacement.
- Purpose: Keeps power physically credible.
- Failure if removed: A weightless heavy opponent erases the contrast.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use position, vaulting or redirected momentum for the agile response, including one recoverable partial contact.
- Purpose: Proves speed without turning the adult into another brute.
- Failure if removed: Equal-force punches collapse the asymmetric design.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let the maximum missed strike damage the established surface and reveal the adult outside its line.
- Purpose: Proves both power and survival in one consequence.
- Failure if removed: A clean miss with no damage undersells the opponent.
