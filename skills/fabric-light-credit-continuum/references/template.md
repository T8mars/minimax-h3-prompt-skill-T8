# 织物光影职员表｜连续载体、球形压痕与字幕接力 Template

## Mechanism

只用一条连续可追踪的悬浮织物作为字幕载体，让移动光球、阴影、条纹切片与柔性褶皱依次改变文字的显隐和尺度；每条职员表都绑定一次可见材质变形，最后织物退出黑场而不混入无关画面。 The reusable mechanism preserves causal order, spatial anchors and the final proof while replacing every source-specific person, location, object, wording, choreography and design.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: One continuous flexible carrier remains identifiable from opening through the final title.
- Purpose: Establishes the baseline identity, object or geography that every later state must preserve.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact baseline rule, later changes cannot be attributed to the same subject, object or place, so the sequence loses its reference state. Removed rule: "One continuous flexible carrier remains identifiable from opening through the final title."

### inv-02

- Rule: Every credit appears only after one visible light, pressure, fold or occlusion event acts on that carrier.
- Purpose: Makes the visible trigger precede and explain the first mechanism response.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact trigger rule, the response appears spontaneously and the central cause-and-effect claim is no longer demonstrated. Removed rule: "Every credit appears only after one visible light, pressure, fold or occlusion event acts on that carrier."

### inv-03

- Rule: Deformation may distort text transiently but each exact credit must recover to a readable state.
- Purpose: Keeps the middle progression registered instead of allowing an unexplained reset.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact progression rule, the middle can jump to a replacement state and the accumulated result is no longer traceable. Removed rule: "Deformation may distort text transiently but each exact credit must recover to a readable state."

### inv-04

- Rule: Color and graphic overlays never replace the underlying cloth or introduce a second unrelated scene.
- Purpose: Reconnects the changed or partial state to the established subject and spatial anchor.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact reconnection rule, detail or transformed shots can belong to a different subject or geography and continuity cannot be verified. Removed rule: "Color and graphic overlays never replace the underlying cloth or introduce a second unrelated scene."

### inv-05

- Rule: The terminal title is held on the released carrier before the same material exits into black.
- Purpose: Turns the terminal hold into observable proof that the mechanism completed.
- Failure if removed: Without this exact terminal-proof rule, the sequence stops on motion alone and never confirms the promised outcome or preserved aftermath. Removed rule: "The terminal title is held on the released carrier before the same material exits into black."


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- One continuous flexible carrier remains identifiable from opening through the final title.
- Every credit appears only after one visible light, pressure, fold or occlusion event acts on that carrier.
- Color and graphic overlays never replace the underlying cloth or introduce a second unrelated scene.
- The terminal title is held on the released carrier before the same material exits into black.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- all source faces, bodies, wardrobe, identities and creator-specific character designs
- the source location, set dressing, props, products and exact environmental geometry
- all source words, logos, watermarks, typefaces and brand identifiers
- the source choreography, exact camera path, shot durations and transition timing
- the source palette, signature effects, prompt wording, creator handle and narrative particulars
