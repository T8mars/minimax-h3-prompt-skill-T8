# Evidence Ladder Crosses into Reality Template

## Mechanism

A bounded evidence carrier first contradicts a stable live scene, then updates the same spatial, temporal, or material variable in two increasingly urgent states before the represented change crosses its boundary into shared reality. Repeated verification, invariant geometry, and delayed performance make the transition legible.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish a live baseline and one bounded evidence carrier that disagree about the same place, object, or state.
- Purpose: Creates a falsifiable question instead of arbitrary surreal imagery.
- Failure if removed: Without a shared referent, the carrier can be predictive, stale, mistaken, or merely decorative.

### inv-02

- Rule: Update one countable variable inside the carrier at least twice while preserving recognizable anchors.
- Purpose: Creates a directional escalation ladder.
- Failure if removed: One change is only a twist; changing every detail destroys comparison.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep the live scene stable long enough for the mismatch to be checked before physical crossing.
- Purpose: Protects negative space and lets the audience learn the rule.
- Failure if removed: Immediate reality change turns the carrier into decoration and the clip into generic transformation VFX.

### inv-04

- Rule: Cross the boundary only after the nearest or most consequential evidence state, then show one physical consequence.
- Purpose: Converts information tension into a concrete payoff.
- Failure if removed: No crossing is unresolved; early crossing removes the ladder; many consequences weaken proof.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let verification and recognition follow the evidence hierarchy.
- Purpose: Synchronizes performance with audience comprehension.
- Failure if removed: Premature fear or wonder tells the audience what to feel before the rule is legible.

## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| live_baseline | Stable place or system with reusable visual anchors. |
| evidence_carrier | Bounded sensor, map, recording, sample, or display. |
| advancing_variable | One countable state that changes twice. |
| verification_action | Repeated check that compares carrier and live scene. |
| boundary_crossing | One legible represented-to-physical transition. |
| emotional_register | New genre-specific feeling. |
| camera_grammar | Original coverage based on information order. |
| sound_family | Carrier, update, reset, and payoff cues. |

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- Do not reconstruct the source camera, instant-print device, abandoned interior, corridor threat, source performer, or final fear close-up.
- Do not copy its exact cool palette, flash rhythm, cut points, approach distances, wording, or signature reveal.
- For any adjacent genre, change carrier modality, location class, subject role, advancing variable, camera grammar, sound family, and payoff physics together.

## Transfer Test

1. Fill every slot.
2. State the shared referent in one sentence.
3. List the two carrier updates as before/after comparisons.
4. Explain why reality remains stable until the payoff.
5. Explain the single boundary crossing and visible consequence.
6. Reject any version that still reads as “different creature in different photograph.”

## Quality Repairs

- **Carrier content changes randomly.** Lock all anchors and vary only one countable property.
- **Reality reacts before the second update.** Hold the baseline until two comparisons are readable.
- **Performance overwhelms the proof.** Delay the strongest reaction and finish on one consequence.
- **The concept is a photograph-horror reskin.** Change modality, genre, location, physics, camera, and emotional payoff together.

## Non-Binding Transfer Examples

- A greenhouse leaf scanner prints two advancing bloom states before a real flowering wave reaches the caretaker's palm.
- A mechanical weather plotter tightens the same pressure curve twice before real polarized wind passes through the station instruments.
- A kiln-resonance tile produces two closer water patterns before vibration aligns loose clay beads into one physical ring.
