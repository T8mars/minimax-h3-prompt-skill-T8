# 未来证据｜异常闯入现实 Template

## Mechanism

A bounded evidence carrier first contradicts a stable live scene, then updates the same spatial variable in two increasingly urgent states before the represented change crosses its boundary into shared reality. Repeated verification, invariant geometry and delayed performance make the transition legible; the carrier, genre, phenomenon, location, camera grammar and emotional payoff remain variable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish a live baseline and one bounded evidence carrier that disagree about the same place, object or state.
- Purpose: Creates a falsifiable question instead of arbitrary surreal imagery.
- Failure if removed: Without a clear baseline or shared referent, the audience cannot tell whether the evidence is predictive, stale, mistaken or merely decorative.

### inv-02

- Rule: Update one countable spatial, temporal or material variable inside the evidence at least twice while preserving recognizable anchors.
- Purpose: Turns a single surprise into an escalation ladder whose direction can be read instantly.
- Failure if removed: One unexplained change is only a twist; changing every detail at once destroys causal comparison.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep the live scene stable long enough for the evidence mismatch to be checked before any physical crossing occurs.
- Purpose: Protects negative space and prevents the payoff from arriving before the rule is understood.
- Failure if removed: If reality changes immediately, the evidence carrier loses its special role and the clip becomes ordinary transformation VFX.

### inv-04

- Rule: Cross the evidence boundary only after the escalation has reached its nearest or most consequential state, and show one unambiguous physical consequence.
- Purpose: Converts information tension into a concrete payoff without requiring a copied monster or attack.
- Failure if removed: No crossing leaves the story unresolved; an early crossing removes the ladder; multiple simultaneous consequences weaken the proof.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let the subject's verification and delayed recognition follow the evidence hierarchy rather than performing fear or wonder before the audience can see the cause.
- Purpose: Synchronizes performance with comprehension and keeps the carrier as the narrative engine.
- Failure if removed: Premature reaction feels melodramatic and tells the audience what to feel before the visual rule is legible.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| live_baseline | One stable place or system with clear anchors that can be checked repeatedly. |
| evidence_carrier | A bounded sensor, map, recording, material sample or display; it must not reuse the source camera or instant photograph. |
| advancing_variable | One countable distance, quantity, color, crack, waveform or growth state that changes twice. |
| verification_action | A repeated human or mechanical check that compares evidence with the live baseline. |
| boundary_crossing | One physically legible moment when the represented state affects the live scene. |
| emotional_register | Horror, wonder, comedy, tenderness or technical urgency derived from the new concept. |
| camera_grammar | Original coverage whose information order differs from table-to-corridor-to-print-to-eye close-ups. |
| sound_family | A designed hierarchy of carrier cues, update cues, brief reset and physical payoff. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 现实基线稳定
- 同一证据载体至少两次更新
- 异常先在证据内出现
- 跨界延迟发生且只有一次克制反应

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- old instant camera, developing print, abandoned photo room, broken window and nested dark corridor
- young male source appearance, tall shadow person, over-shoulder corridor reveal and extreme terrified-eye ending
- source cold blue-black palette, exact flash rhythm, exact cut boundaries and exact approach distances
- phrases and concepts such as future camera, photograph the future, instant photograph and tall shadow figure

## Transfer Tests

1. In a moonlit botanical greenhouse, a caretaker uses a brass leaf scanner whose narrow paper strip shows a luminous flowering wave advancing through an otherwise dark planting bed; after two scans, the real bloom travels through soil and opens in her palm.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: live_baseline, evidence_carrier, advancing_variable, verification_action, boundary_crossing, emotional_register, camera_grammar, sound_family
2. At a remote weather station, a mechanical sky plotter draws a colored pressure ribbon approaching the building while the clear horizon remains unchanged; successive plots tighten the curve until real wind and polarized light sweep through the instruments.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: live_baseline, evidence_carrier, advancing_variable, verification_action, boundary_crossing, emotional_register, camera_grammar, sound_family
