# 微缩多材质路线｜动作动词接力到高点终点 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult miniature traveler, at least two near scale anchors, three materially different route zones and one elevated destination that is visible or spatially recoverable from the opening. Move the traveler through every zone in a continuous objective direction, and require each material to change the locomotion verb—such as stepping, balancing, sliding, climbing or sprinting. The landing or recovery from one zone must physically hand off to the next; do not reset identity, wardrobe or route. Reach the previously established elevated destination through a visible final ascent and hold a wide terminal view that includes the adult and at least two prior material families. The reusable mechanism is miniature scale proof, multi-material locomotion relay, continuous destination logic, action-to-action handoff and elevated route closure—not the source person, face, clothing, breakfast food, syrup, cereal, pancake, kitchen, palette, exact terrain order, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult miniature traveler, at least two near scale anchors, three material zones and one elevated destination before the route escalates.
- Purpose: Makes both miniature scale and the promised endpoint recoverable.
- Failure if removed: Without a destination and scale baseline, the sequence becomes unrelated macro vignettes.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give every material zone a different locomotion verb.
- Purpose: Lets physical properties—not decoration—drive progression.
- Failure if removed: Running identically across every surface makes material changes functionally meaningless.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use the landing or recovery from one zone as the physical entry into the next while preserving objective direction.
- Purpose: Builds a continuous causal route across discontinuous textures.
- Failure if removed: Resetting between terrains turns the adventure into a montage.

### inv-04

- Rule: Preserve adult identity, wardrobe and at least one recurring route cue through every scale change.
- Purpose: Keeps the same traveler and geography verifiable.
- Failure if removed: A tiny silhouette alone cannot prove continuity after major environment changes.

### inv-05

- Rule: Reach the established high destination through a visible final ascent and hold a wide view containing at least two prior material families.
- Purpose: Proves arrival and route completion instead of merely posing on a new object.
- Failure if removed: A sudden summit cut breaks the promised traversal.
