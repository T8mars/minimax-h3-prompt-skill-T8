# 触觉旅行章法｜近物接触到高位全景递进 Template

## Mechanism

Bind one original traveler through at least four geographically distinct chapters using stable identity, wardrobe and forward direction. In each chapter, require one brief hand, body or tool contact with a location-specific material before moving on. Order the chapters so spatial scale expands from constrained passage and near object, through a dense social field and moving background geography, to a final climb and elevated overview. Apply one consistent archival medium system across all locations. The reusable mechanism is tactile chapter binding, scale expansion and ascent payoff—not the source traveler, Istanbul, blue blouse, cream skirt, leather bag, wooden door, oranges, spice market, ferry, flower, stone stairs, skyline, exact film stock, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Keep one traveler identity, wardrobe anchor and forward route direction across all chapters.
- Purpose: Makes separate locations read as one continuous memory journey.
- Failure if removed: Identity or direction resets turn the piece into unrelated postcards.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give every location one brief physical contact with a local material or object.
- Purpose: Creates embodied evidence and chapter-specific memory anchors.
- Failure if removed: Pure sightseeing produces generic scenic montage.

### inv-03

- Rule: Expand spatial scale from constrained passage through social and geographic breadth to an elevated overview.
- Purpose: Builds a readable progression rather than equal-weight clips.
- Failure if removed: Random scale order removes the final destination payoff.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use one consistent archival-medium rule across all chapters.
- Purpose: Binds lighting and location changes into one remembered document.
- Failure if removed: Changing defects or render media per cut fragments the archive.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve the route through a climb and stable high-position hold.
- Purpose: Turns movement into a destination with visible geographic understanding.
- Failure if removed: Ending mid-route or on another close object leaves no culmination.

- the source Istanbul streets, wooden door, fruit and spice market, oranges, waterfront, ferry, flowers, stone stairs and skyline

- the source visible people, signage, boats, urban layout and location-specific architecture
