# 连续拉远尺度揭危｜小型载体、扩张暗区与临界停留 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original small moving carrier with a readable heading, speed cue and trail before an environmental anomaly is legible. Use one continuous camera retreat and elevation change with no reveal cut, keeping the carrier and anomaly in the same coordinate relationship as more environment enters the frame. Let the anomaly evolve from an ambiguous dark or geometric texture into a coherent hazard that occupies progressively more of the composition, while the carrier remains the stable scale unit. End on a held pre-impact or threshold state before the carrier disappears. The reusable mechanism is moving scale anchor, uninterrupted context expansion, ambiguity-to-hazard reveal and critical-distance hold—not the source wooden ship, sail, ocean, whirlpool, Odyssey claim, exact camera path, water physics, palette, watermark, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one small moving carrier with heading, speed cue and trail before the anomaly is legible.
- Purpose: Provides a stable comparison unit for every later scale reveal.
- Failure if removed: Without a moving anchor, the hazard's size and distance remain abstract.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one uninterrupted camera retreat and elevation change with no reveal cut.
- Purpose: Lets scale emerge from added context rather than editorial substitution.
- Failure if removed: A cut can fake scale and break the promised continuous observation.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep carrier and anomaly in one coordinate relationship as the frame expands.
- Purpose: Makes approach distance and danger continuously measurable.
- Failure if removed: Reframing them independently removes spatial causality.

### inv-04

- Rule: Grow the anomaly from ambiguous texture into a coherent hazard occupying more of the frame.
- Purpose: Turns curiosity into threat through visual evidence alone.
- Failure if removed: Showing the full hazard immediately removes the escalation.

### inv-05

- Rule: Hold before impact or disappearance with the carrier still readable.
- Purpose: Preserves the final scale comparison and avoids replacing tension with destruction.
- Failure if removed: Losing the carrier prevents verification of the critical distance.
