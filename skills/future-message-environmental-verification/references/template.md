# 未来来信｜私密警告被外部世界兑现 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with a private record that is visibly dated or sourced from a future version of the subject. Keep its message bounded inside the proof device, then make the present subject move to one stable external aperture for verification. Reveal a familiar environment in an impossible future state, and only after that first confirmation introduce a much larger external anomaly that reframes the warning as an immediate unresolved threat.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one private proof record with a visibly impossible future origin.
- Purpose: Creates a falsifiable information anomaly.
- Failure if removed: A vague mysterious message lacks temporal proof.

### inv-02

- Rule: Bind the record to a future version or unmistakable continuation of the present subject.
- Purpose: Makes verification personally motivated.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated messenger weakens the identity bridge.

### inv-03

- Rule: Move the present subject from the private record to one stable external aperture.
- Purpose: Turns information into an observable test.
- Failure if removed: A cut directly to spectacle removes the verification action.

### inv-04

- Rule: First reveal a familiar environment in a clearly altered future state.
- Purpose: Provides a local confirmation before escalation.
- Failure if removed: Skipping local confirmation makes the warning ungrounded.

### inv-05

- Rule: Introduce one larger external anomaly only after the environment confirms the time shift, and end unresolved.
- Purpose: Escalates evidence into threat without overexplaining it.
- Failure if removed: An early giant reveal consumes the evidence ladder.

## Transfer Tests

1. At an original polar observatory, a young cartographer opens a sealed frost-cylinder stamped with a date eighty years ahead and sees an elderly version of herself in a tiny volumetric archive. She crosses to the dome slit, finds the familiar ice shelf replaced by luminous towers, then a continent-scale ring shadow rotates across the aurora.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. In handcrafted paper stop motion, an orchard keeper receives a seed envelope printed with her own future portrait and a harvest year not yet reached. She opens the barn shutter, sees mature glass trees occupying the valley, then their crowns align into one enormous mechanical canopy above the farm.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
