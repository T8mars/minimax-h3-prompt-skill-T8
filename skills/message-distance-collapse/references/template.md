# 信息逼近｜远端警告压缩到当前空间 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one subject inside stable, easily rechecked geography. Deliver a first bounded message with an ambiguous spatial warning, let the subject inspect the indicated area and find no confirmed source, then deliver a second message that precisely locates the sender inside the subject's immediate blind spot. End on the subject's delayed reaction or initial turn without revealing the sender.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish stable local geography before any message changes its meaning.
- Purpose: Makes distance claims verifiable.
- Failure if removed: Abstract darkness provides no spatial reference.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one bounded first message that warns about a direction but keeps the sender remote or ambiguous.
- Purpose: Creates a cautious information hook.
- Failure if removed: An immediate close-proximity claim skips the ladder.

### inv-03

- Rule: Let the subject visibly inspect the indicated area without confirming a threat.
- Purpose: Preserves uncertainty through active verification.
- Failure if removed: Showing the sender early collapses suspense.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use a second message to locate the sender inside the subject's current blind spot.
- Purpose: Performs the decisive distance collapse.
- Failure if removed: A repeated vague warning does not escalate spatially.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on a held reaction or beginning turn before the sender is revealed.
- Purpose: Keeps attention on information becoming physical risk.
- Failure if removed: A full creature reveal replaces the mechanism with a jump scare.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source blonde woman and cream sweater
- the source warm residential hallway and dark doorway
- the source smartphone call interface
- DON'T LOOK. LOOK BEHIND YOU.
- I'M STANDING RIGHT BEHIND YOU.
- the source exact face-phone-hallway alternation

## Transfer Tests

1. At an original radio telescope, a night technician receives two dot-matrix maintenance strips. The first says the north dish should remain dark; she checks the empty dish through a control slit. The second reports that her shadow is now pointing toward the console; a second shadow edge appears beside hers and she starts to turn before cut.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. In stop-motion paper theatre, a lighthouse keeper receives a wax-sealed signal card warning that the lower lantern is unoccupied. He checks the empty stairwell; a second card says the lantern has moved into his room. Its light sweeps across the wall behind him as he turns, without revealing the lantern.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
