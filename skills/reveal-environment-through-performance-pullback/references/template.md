# 表演让位于环境｜近脸到全景的连续退镜 Template

## Mechanism

Begin in an identity-close performance frame, keep the performance continuous while one unbroken pullback gradually transfers visual dominance from face to body and then to a responsive environment, and end with the performer still readable inside the now-dominant wide setting.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Open close enough to establish one stable performer identity.
- Purpose: Gives the later scale transfer a human anchor.
- Failure if removed: Opening wide makes the performer secondary too early.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep one continuous performance thread through the camera retreat.
- Purpose: Prevents the pullback from becoming scenery montage.
- Failure if removed: Stopping performance breaks the causal link.

### inv-03

- Rule: Reveal the first environmental response before the frame becomes wide.
- Purpose: Connects performer and setting gradually.
- Failure if removed: A sudden final background replacement feels unrelated.

### inv-04

- Rule: Transfer visual dominance through camera distance rather than a subject transformation.
- Purpose: Makes the scale shift legible and reusable.
- Failure if removed: Changing the performer instead of the frame changes the mechanism.

### inv-05

- Rule: End wide with both performer and responsive environment readable.
- Purpose: Completes the relationship without losing identity.
- Failure if removed: A landscape-only ending discards the opening anchor.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| causal_carrier | One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain. |
| state_change | Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Open close enough to establish one stable performer identity.
- Keep one continuous performance thread through the camera retreat.
- Transfer visual dominance through camera distance rather than a subject transformation.
- End wide with both performer and responsive environment readable.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source female singer
- source shoreline and aurora
- source translucent dress and face lighting
- the exact vocal-performance pullback

## Transfer Tests

1. A glass-bowl artisan begins in a close face-and-hands frame; one continuous retreat reveals resonating bowls, then a bioluminescent greenhouse canopy responding in expanding rings while the artisan keeps circling one mallet.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A weather engineer begins close while turning one tuning dial; the camera retreats through a tower chamber as suspended fabric vanes respond one by one, ending with the engineer small beneath the full wind lattice.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
