# 地貌变形揭示｜局部异常到巨体逼近 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with an environmental disturbance that could still be mistaken for terrain, reveal one identifying feature before the full body, switch to a high or wide view that proves the complete geometry and its scale effect on the environment, then return to a lower viewpoint for one final proximity breach.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Open on an environmental deformation that can still be mistaken for terrain.
- Purpose: Creates uncertainty before identification.
- Failure if removed: A full creature opening removes the reveal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Reveal one identifying feature while withholding most of the body.
- Purpose: Confirms agency without spending scale.
- Failure if removed: Several features at once collapse the ladder.

### inv-03

- Rule: Extend the partial form enough to establish directional body geometry.
- Purpose: Bridges identity to full-scale proof.
- Failure if removed: Jumping directly to the overhead body feels disconnected.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use one high or wide view to prove the complete geometry and its environmental consequence.
- Purpose: Makes scale measurable.
- Failure if removed: A close body texture gives no spatial proof.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return to a lower viewpoint for one bounded proximity breach.
- Purpose: Converts distant scale into immediate stakes.
- Failure if removed: Ending only overhead remains an illustration.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| causal_carrier | One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain. |
| state_change | Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Open on an environmental deformation that can still be mistaken for terrain.
- Reveal one identifying feature while withholding most of the body.
- Use one high or wide view to prove the complete geometry and its environmental consequence.
- Return to a lower viewpoint for one bounded proximity breach.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source ocean serpent
- green glowing eyes and storm sea
- source whirlpool coil and open jaws
- the exact sea-creature reveal order

## Transfer Tests

1. A wind-buried walking greenhouse first appears as a ridge under wheat, one amber inspection window opens, articulated roof ribs rise, an aerial view proves its vast footprint bending crop lanes, and a low road view ends as one leg steps across the foreground without contact.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A subterranean archive machine first appears as a shifting library floor, one blue lens opens between shelves, a curved chassis emerges, a ceiling view proves a spiral body moving book aisles, and a low desk view ends with its scanner passing close overhead.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
