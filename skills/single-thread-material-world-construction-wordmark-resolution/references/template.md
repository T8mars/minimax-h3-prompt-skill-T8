# 单线织世｜一根材料分叉建景再回收到原创标记 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with one original material filament on a clearly readable substrate. Every new branch, color, structure and landscape region must grow from that still-traceable parent network; preserve junctions and paths as provenance. Escalate from macro material proof to at least three distinct but connected world regions. At peak density, reverse the same network so peripheral branches travel back along established paths and reorganize into one original short name or emblem. The reusable mechanism is single-origin material proof, branching construction, connected regional escalation, reverse collection and wordmark resolution—not the source textile, colors, castles, terrains, logo, creator name, exact camera or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one material filament and one substrate before any world appears.
- Purpose: Provides a physical origin for every later element.
- Failure if removed: Multiple unexplained sources turn construction into arbitrary morphing.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep the parent network and every major junction traceable as branches construct new regions.
- Purpose: Preserves provenance and material continuity.
- Failure if removed: Hidden cuts make the world look pre-existing rather than built.

### inv-03

- Rule: Grow at least three visually distinct regions from connected branches while sharing one substrate.
- Purpose: Creates breadth without abandoning unity.
- Failure if removed: One repeated region cannot prove the construction system is reusable.

### inv-04

- Rule: At peak density, reverse movement along already established paths instead of dissolving the world.
- Purpose: Makes the return causally symmetric with the build.
- Failure if removed: A generic fade loses material conservation.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve into one new original short name or emblem and hold it clearly.
- Purpose: Converts complexity into a legible identity payoff.
- Failure if removed: Copying the source name or ending on loose threads fails the transfer.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source dark cloth, thread colors, weave and embroidery texture
- source castles, mountains, desert, snow, rivers and exact terrain layout
- source branching paths, construction order and exact camera climb
- source creator name, Koda-like wordmark and logo geometry
- source MiniMax H3 wording, timing and creator identity
