# 环境前沿｜局部点燃到全域占据 Template

## Mechanism

Environmental escalation is legible when one contiguous front grows through a fixed geography and each phase occupies a larger measurable area. Static anchors must remain visible long enough to prove propagation, and the final wide state should show that the environment—not merely exposure or color grading—has materially changed.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish a bounded environment, an untouched region and one localized starting front.
- Purpose: Defines the area available for change.
- Failure if removed: Opening on a fully transformed field removes progression.

### inv-02

- Rule: Grow one contiguous front instead of spawning unrelated effects everywhere.
- Purpose: Preserves causal propagation.
- Failure if removed: Random flashes create spectacle without a readable process.

### inv-03

- Rule: Cross at least two fixed spatial anchors in order.
- Purpose: Turns expansion into measurable progress.
- Failure if removed: Without anchors, apparent growth may be only camera movement.

### inv-04

- Rule: Increase occupied area and material state, not just brightness.
- Purpose: Proves a physical environmental change.
- Failure if removed: Exposure shifts can imitate escalation without propagation.

### inv-05

- Rule: Hold a final wide comparison showing transformed and residual untouched regions.
- Purpose: Completes the area proof.
- Failure if removed: A tight climax cannot verify site-wide change.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Establish a bounded environment, an untouched region and one localized starting front.
- Grow one contiguous front instead of spawning unrelated effects everywhere.
- Increase occupied area and material state, not just brightness.
- Hold a final wide comparison showing transformed and residual untouched regions.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- dark battlefield terrain
- large silhouetted creature
- orange fire waves
- source ignition layout
- source battle and flame climax

## Transfer Tests

1. Inside a circular aquarium laboratory, one palm-sized blue algae bloom begins near an intake vent, crosses three numbered coral gates, expands into branching luminous sheets and ends in a wide overhead view that shows most of the tank transformed while one clear water pocket remains.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. Across a frosted greenhouse window, a warm condensation-clear front begins at one heater patch, crosses two metal mullions, reveals leaf silhouettes in increasing area and ends on a wide window comparison with one untouched frosted corner.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
