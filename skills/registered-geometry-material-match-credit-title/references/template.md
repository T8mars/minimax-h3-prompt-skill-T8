# 几何注册匹配片头｜平面路径、实体面板与终局印记 Template

## Mechanism

Establish an original planar geometry with at least three stable registration intersections and one replaceable color anchor. Let one tool action reveal a short original credit without losing the geometry, then match the same intersections into a materially different physical surface at the exact screen coordinates and reveal a second credit through light or shadow. Reintroduce the color anchor around one original motif object, cause one tactile material transformation and let that material fill the frame before resolving a large original master title. The reusable mechanism is registered geometry, material match cut, motif consolidation and physical-to-flat title closure—not the source maze, paper, pen, perforated wall, key, wax seal, names, title, red-black palette, exact typography, hands, composition, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish at least three stable geometry intersections and one color anchor before the first credit.
- Purpose: Creates measurable registration points for the later material match.
- Failure if removed: A loose pattern cannot support a convincing cross-material join.

### inv-02

- Rule: Reveal one short original credit through a tool action while preserving the geometry.
- Purpose: Binds typography to physical cause instead of overlaying unrelated text.
- Failure if removed: If the carrier disappears, the credit cannot hand off to the next surface.

### inv-03

- Rule: Match the same intersections into a materially different surface at exact screen coordinates.
- Purpose: Makes two independently generated segments read as one continuous title system.
- Failure if removed: Approximate framing produces a visible jump instead of a designed seam.

### inv-04

- Rule: Return the color anchor around one original motif object and perform one tactile transformation.
- Purpose: Consolidates the earlier graphic clues into a terminal physical event.
- Failure if removed: A new unrelated motif makes the final title feel detached.

### inv-05

- Rule: Fill the frame with the transformed material and hold one large original master title.
- Purpose: Converts physical motion into a readable final lockup.
- Failure if removed: Continuing action after the title prevents the sequence from resolving.
