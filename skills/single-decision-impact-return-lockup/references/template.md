# 单次决策兑现｜准备—执行—后果—回归 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one dominant external threat, isolate a responsible actor through a dedicated preparation phase, make the decision legible through a visible calibration or commitment cue, execute exactly one decisive action, show its material consequence, then return to the responsible actor or outcome owner before a final lockup. The mechanism depends on causal proof, not on combat, weapons or destruction.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one dominant external threat or failure state before the actor commits.
- Purpose: Gives the sequence one legible problem.
- Failure if removed: Without an opening threat, preparation becomes generic spectacle.

### inv-02

- Rule: Separate preparation from execution with one visible readiness action.
- Purpose: Makes commitment auditable.
- Failure if removed: Immediate action removes the feeling of a deliberate decision.

### inv-03

- Rule: Insert one calibration, alignment or confirmation cue immediately before the decisive action.
- Purpose: Proves why the action occurs at that moment.
- Failure if removed: A missing confirmation cue turns execution into an arbitrary cut.

### inv-04

- Rule: Execute one decisive action and show one directly attributable material consequence.
- Purpose: Completes the causal promise.
- Failure if removed: Multiple unrelated actions or an unseen result dissolve accountability.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return to the actor or outcome owner and hold a final lockup after the consequence.
- Purpose: Confirms who owns the result and closes the arc.
- Failure if removed: Ending only on the effect abandons the decision-maker and weakens closure.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source armored woman and shoulder launcher
- the source ruined futuristic warzone and approaching aircraft
- the source eye-targeting HUD and projectile-follow shot
- the source aircraft fireball and burning skyline
- the source SKYFALL title card and exact threat-preparation-launch-impact order
