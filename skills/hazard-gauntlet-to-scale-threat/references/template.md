# 连续脱险｜假安全后升级为尺度威胁 Template

## Mechanism

Chain several visually distinct local hazards along one continuous escape direction, let the subject repeatedly recover, grant one brief apparent safety upgrade, then reveal a single environmental-scale threat that dwarfs both subject and escape platform.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one subject and one persistent escape direction before the hazard chain.
- Purpose: Binds separate dangers into one route.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated directions make the sequence feel like a montage.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use at least two materially different local hazards with visible recoveries between them.
- Purpose: Builds escalation through changing physical duties.
- Failure if removed: Repeated copies of one obstacle create no meaningful progression.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep the same subject and route logic traceable through every recovery.
- Purpose: Preserves one continuous survival problem.
- Failure if removed: Subject or geography resets break the causal chain.

### inv-04

- Rule: Provide one brief apparent safety upgrade or open platform late in the sequence.
- Purpose: Creates a readable baseline for the final reversal of scale.
- Failure if removed: Without temporary relief, the ending is merely another hazard.

### inv-05

- Rule: Use one terminal scale reveal to reclassify the apparent safety state without inventing survival beyond the visible ending.
- Purpose: Makes the final threat change the meaning of all prior escapes.
- Failure if removed: Claiming an unseen outcome overstates the evidence and weakens the hold.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Establish one subject and one persistent escape direction before the hazard chain.
- Use at least two materially different local hazards with visible recoveries between them.
- Provide one brief apparent safety upgrade or open platform late in the sequence.
- Use one terminal scale reveal to reclassify the apparent safety state without inventing survival beyond the visible ending.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source young woman runner
- shipping containers and industrial yard
- cars, pipes and yard-specific hazards
- white yacht
- giant shark and ocean whirlpool
- the exact source escape choreography
