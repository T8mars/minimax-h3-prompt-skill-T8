# 产品环境提取回环｜材质微距、原料世界、喷雾门户与透明球归返 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original product through macro material anchors before showing the complete object. Connect at least two visible ingredient or design details to one coherent environment identity. Use one functional product action as the sole optical transition into the environment. Expand the environment, then enclose its recognizable geometry inside one transparent carrier. Return the carrier and environment to the same complete product with stable shape, label-free identity and hero proof. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one original product through macro material anchors before showing the complete object.
- Purpose: Makes the baseline measurable before the mechanism changes it.
- Failure if removed: Without the baseline, later change has no trustworthy reference state.

### inv-02

- Rule: Connect at least two visible ingredient or design details to one coherent environment identity.
- Purpose: Binds the first visible change to a specific cause rather than decoration.
- Failure if removed: Without a visible trigger, the transition reads as an arbitrary edit.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one functional product action as the sole optical transition into the environment.
- Purpose: Preserves identity, material or spatial continuity through the middle transition.
- Failure if removed: Without continuity, the sequence becomes unrelated montage or identity drift.

### inv-04

- Rule: Expand the environment, then enclose its recognizable geometry inside one transparent carrier.
- Purpose: Raises intensity or information while retaining the same readable grammar.
- Failure if removed: Without controlled escalation, the middle either plateaus or turns into noise.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return the carrier and environment to the same complete product with stable shape, label-free identity and hero proof.
- Purpose: Provides a terminal proof that resolves the mechanism instead of merely stopping.
- Failure if removed: Without terminal proof, the promised result or reversal remains unverified.
