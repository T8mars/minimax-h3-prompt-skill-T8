# 群体登陆｜载体抵达—先锋越界—队列释放 Template

## Mechanism

Bring one group carrier to a clearly visible environmental boundary. Keep a lead subject identifiable as that subject performs the first complete crossing and establishes a viable direction beyond the boundary. Use the leader's continued advance as the cue for an ordered follower release, then widen or elevate the view until carrier, crossing path, leader and group destination are readable together. The reusable mechanism is carrier arrival, pioneer proof, queue release and spatial confirmation, not the source longship, warriors, weapons, shields, storm or historical identity.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one group carrier, one environmental boundary and one identifiable lead subject in the same opening geography.
- Purpose: Defines origin, obstacle and agent before movement.
- Failure if removed: A close action opening cannot prove where the group came from or what must be crossed.

### inv-02

- Rule: Let the lead subject complete the first crossing before the follower group begins its release.
- Purpose: Makes the route proof causal and attributable.
- Failure if removed: Simultaneous mass movement removes the pioneer function.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep the lead subject moving beyond the boundary while the first follower enters the proven path.
- Purpose: Turns solo success into a release signal.
- Failure if removed: If the leader stops or disappears, the queue lacks directional ownership.

### inv-04

- Rule: Release followers in one ordered flow while keeping the carrier and boundary traceable.
- Purpose: Makes group transfer legible instead of chaotic.
- Failure if removed: Detached crowd inserts or reversed flow break the spatial handoff.

### inv-05

- Rule: End from a wider or higher view that shows changed occupancy across carrier, path and destination.
- Purpose: Provides objective completion evidence.
- Failure if removed: Ending on a single hero pose cannot prove the group has transferred.
