# 地面召集天空编队｜近距确认、首领降落与乘骑离场 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult ground caller, one distant aerial group and a stable terrain anchor before any close pass. Let one visible gesture, stance or nonverbal signal change the group's topology from dispersed approach to an ordered orbit or repeated route. Select one dominant carrier for a reciprocal close acknowledgment, then show its complete descent, stable landing and lowered mount posture. Keep hand or body contact readable as the adult mounts, and end only after the pair takes off and joins the broader aerial route. The reusable mechanism is ground-to-sky ordering, group-to-leader acknowledgment, carrier selection, mounting and shared departure—not the source dragons, warrior, armor, staff, coast, choreography, faces, camera route, fantasy style, music, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one adult ground caller, a distant aerial group and one terrain anchor before close passes.
- Purpose: Makes the initial vertical separation and later response measurable.
- Failure if removed: Beginning in a chaotic swarm hides who is calling whom.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one visible nonverbal signal to change the aerial topology from dispersed approach to ordered orbit or route.
- Purpose: Turns spectacle into a causal group response.
- Failure if removed: Unchanged random flight does not prove command or coordination.

### inv-03

- Rule: Select one dominant carrier through reciprocal close acknowledgment.
- Purpose: Narrows group energy into a specific transport relationship.
- Failure if removed: A random mount arriving without recognition feels disconnected.

### inv-04

- Rule: Show complete descent, stable landing, lowered posture and readable mounting contact.
- Purpose: Makes the transport transition physically usable.
- Failure if removed: Teleporting onto the carrier skips the critical handoff.

### inv-05

- Rule: End after the mounted pair takes off and joins the broader aerial route.
- Purpose: Closes ground leadership as shared movement through the sky.
- Failure if removed: Ending at the mount leaves the promised departure incomplete.
