# 世界换材｜主体不断线穿过环境突变 Template

## Mechanism

Lock one moving subject to a legible route, perform one global environmental material conversion around that uninterrupted motion, then prove persistence by continuing toward a visible destination under the new world's rules.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one subject, one travel axis and one ordinary environmental rule before conversion.
- Purpose: Creates a baseline against which the world change can be measured.
- Failure if removed: Beginning inside the converted world removes the causal contrast.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep subject identity, direction and motion continuous through the decisive instant.
- Purpose: Makes the environment, rather than the traveler, the changing system.
- Failure if removed: A motion reset makes the change read as an unrelated scene cut.

### inv-03

- Rule: Convert the environment only once with a visibly global material change.
- Purpose: Concentrates the spectacle into one legible causal event.
- Failure if removed: Several unrelated transformations weaken the governing rule.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use passing geometry or material response to prove the new world persists around the same route.
- Purpose: Provides continuing spatial evidence after the conversion.
- Failure if removed: A single flash frame cannot prove a persistent new world.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on continued travel toward a readable destination under the converted rule, not a reset to the opening world.
- Purpose: Turns persistence into the terminal result.
- Failure if removed: Returning to the original environment cancels the conversion consequence.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |

| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |

| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source mountain biker
- rocky alpine ridge and dirt jump
- transparent glass shard maze
- the source rider wardrobe and bicycle
- the exact cliff-to-glass spectacle and framing
