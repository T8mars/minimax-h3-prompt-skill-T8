# 墨烟试装升级｜日常基线、遮挡换装与黑白合流 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one clearly adult original subject, a readable everyday full-body baseline and one continuously traceable transformation carrier before the first style change. Use each carrier crossing as the visible cause of exactly one declared styling change while preserving face, age, body proportions, pose axis and studio geography. Escalate styling in ordered density levels from practical clothing to tailored and sculptural looks without changing every visual channel at once. Insert one bounded portrait checkpoint that re-verifies stable identity anchors before the terminal full-body phase. Split the carrier into two visibly distinct branches, then recombine them into one stable two-part hero design and hold the completed result. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.
