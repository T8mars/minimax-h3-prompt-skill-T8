# 固定见证框架世界降级｜车厢不变、窗外状态逐级坠落 Template

## Mechanism

Lock the camera inside one original moving carrier with a persistent window frame, interior geometry and at least two clearly adult witness identities. Keep the carrier's movement direction and interior continuity stable while the exterior passes through at least four increasingly impossible world states. Let each state become visually legible through the window before witness performance changes, then escalate reactions from indifference to attention, bracing and silent awe without turning the adults into narrators. Use environmental light spill to bind exterior changes back onto the same interior. End after one final threshold reveals a qualitatively different terminal world while the unchanged carrier and witnesses remain visible. The reusable mechanism is fixed witness frame, stable carrier interior, staged exterior state descent, reaction lag, light-spill coupling and terminal-world arrival—not the source train, passengers, faces, phone look, city, tunnels, lava, hell imagery, exact route, camera, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one camera inside a moving carrier with stable window, interior and at least two adult witness anchors.
- Purpose: Creates a trusted continuous reality frame.
- Failure if removed: Exterior-only cuts cannot prove one witnessed journey.

### inv-02

- Rule: Transform only the exterior through at least four ordered world states while carrier direction remains constant.
- Purpose: Turns the window into a controlled state machine.
- Failure if removed: Interior resets or random directions break continuity.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show each exterior state clearly before witness reactions increase.
- Purpose: Lets evidence cause performance rather than the reverse.
- Failure if removed: Premature reactions feel scripted and ungrounded.

### inv-04

- Rule: Couple exterior escalation to changing light spill across the unchanged interior.
- Purpose: Physically binds both layers into one event.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated color grading makes the window look composited.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with a qualitatively new terminal world and the same witnesses still inside the carrier.
- Purpose: Confirms irreversible travel rather than a visual loop.
- Failure if removed: Removing the carrier at the end loses the witness contract.
