# 时装世界节拍轮转｜同一成年角色跨风格、静默与英雄收束 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one clearly adult original fashion subject with at least three stable identity anchors. Rotate distinct style modules while changing only declared wardrobe, accessory, palette and typography variables. Bind every module to subject action, camera movement and foreground motion while preserving identity. Insert one authored low-density pause after the maximum-intensity phase. Return prior modules as a compressed recall and finish on the same adult in one stable hero frame. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult original fashion subject with at least three stable identity anchors.

- Rule: Rotate distinct style modules while changing only declared wardrobe, accessory, palette and typography variables.

### inv-03

- Rule: Bind every module to subject action, camera movement and foreground motion while preserving identity.
- Purpose: Preserves identity, material or spatial continuity through the middle transition.
- Failure if removed: Without continuity, the sequence becomes unrelated montage or identity drift.

### inv-04

- Rule: Insert one authored low-density pause after the maximum-intensity phase.
- Purpose: Raises intensity or information while retaining the same readable grammar.
- Failure if removed: Without controlled escalation, the middle either plateaus or turns into noise.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return prior modules as a compressed recall and finish on the same adult in one stable hero frame.
- Purpose: Provides a terminal proof that resolves the mechanism instead of merely stopping.
