# 平面重组｜几何节奏品牌片 Template

## Mechanism

Build a flat poster world around one dominant geometric motif and one stable protagonist silhouette. Repeatedly fracture and reconstruct the layout through hard-edged crops, diagrams, negative space and key states; reserve continuous physical action, then slow briefly for a contrastive operational tableau and resolve all graphic families into one closing system. Motif, palette, subject class, icon family, tempo, contrast state and final graphic outcome are variable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one dominant geometric motif and protagonist silhouette immediately.
- Purpose: Creates anchors for later fragmentation.
- Failure if removed: Without anchors, rapid recomposition becomes random collage.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep all space flat and poster-like while graphic planes transform.
- Purpose: Protects the editorial MG identity.
- Failure if removed: Realistic 3D depth breaks the visual system.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use key states and symbolic environmental inserts instead of continuous combat.
- Purpose: Lets composition carry motion and preserves readability.
- Failure if removed: Full action choreography competes with graphic transitions.

### inv-04

- Rule: Peak information density before a short contrastive operational tableau.
- Purpose: Creates a clear acceleration and breath without requiring a rival character.
- Failure if removed: Uniform density removes rhythm and makes the operational state unreadable.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve the established graphic families into one original closing system.
- Purpose: Converts montage energy into a memorable endpoint.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated logo or fade fails to resolve the system.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original subject whose identity and silhouette remain stable. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| escalation_variable | One readable variable that changes monotonically or in countable stages. |
| performance_rule | One restrained performance rule that makes the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | A coherent information order that does not copy the source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | One clean physical or graphic consequence that closes the causal chain. |
| sound_family | A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting each state change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 只有一个几何母题
- 主体剪影稳定
- 拆解与重组多次发生
- 高密后留短暂停顿并用既有图形收束

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- giant purple lunar eclipse and lunar phase diagrams
- black-haired woman with massive katana
- gray-haired villain in a wide-brim black hat
- source purple-black-silver palette, raven inserts and exact astrology UI

## Transfer Tests

   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A weather-station ident centers a wind-rosette motif, sensor-mast silhouette and pressure maps, slows on an empty storm shutter state, then resolves into a route-bearing forecast system.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
