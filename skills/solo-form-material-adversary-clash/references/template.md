# 独演成敌｜动作材质凝成对手后碰撞 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with one subject performing a complete, readable form that establishes both a movement signature and a material trace. Let environmental residue from that performance accumulate before it condenses into an independent adversary; the opponent must visibly originate from the established material family. Use one spatial crossing to bring both agents into a shared confrontation, introduce a contrasting second material only at the late peak, and close on a readable subject state after the material collision.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one subject's complete movement signature and its material trace before an opponent appears.
- Purpose: Creates the action vocabulary and origin material.
- Failure if removed: An immediate enemy gives the material no causal history.

### inv-02

- Rule: Accumulate the same environmental material around the subject before it gains agency.
- Purpose: Makes the adversary an outcome of the established field.
- Failure if removed: A random arriving character is only an unrelated antagonist.

### inv-03

- Rule: Condense the material into an independent opponent and bring both agents onto one readable spatial route.
- Purpose: Converts solo proof into a causal confrontation.
- Failure if removed: Abstract weather without an opponent cannot test the movement signature.

### inv-04

- Rule: Reserve a contrasting second material for one late clash peak.
- Purpose: Creates a measurable escalation threshold.
- Failure if removed: Using both materials from the start flattens the hierarchy.

### inv-05

- Rule: After the collision, restore one readable subject state with the signature action or tool still identifiable.
- Purpose: Closes the arc on agency rather than pure spectacle.
- Failure if removed: Ending inside debris leaves the causal winner and retained skill unclear.
