# 追逐路线崩塌再解释｜逃生载体毁灭后目标异地重现 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult protagonist alone at a legible departure node, one approaching group and one apparently decisive escape carrier. Use a continuous directional chase to bind the adult's objective to boarding that carrier, then give boarding a brief readable false-resolution hold. Destroy or permanently disable only the apparent route in one visually decisive event while withholding the protagonist's state through smoke, occlusion or a clean temporal cut. Reintroduce the same adult at a geographically separate safe vantage with at least three identity anchors intact, and hold a deliberate look back toward the former route or camera long enough to prove survival. The reusable mechanism is isolation, closing pursuit, carrier-bound false escape, irreversible route collapse, withheld status and remote survivor reframe—not the source woman, face, wet hair, clothing, subway, station, pursuers, explosion, city roof, exact shots, title text, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Open with one clearly adult original protagonist, one approaching group and one visible escape carrier in a readable departure geography.
- Purpose: Creates a single apparent objective and pressure vector.
- Failure if removed: Without all three anchors, later boarding and route loss have no causal meaning.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep pursuit direction continuous until the protagonist boards the carrier.
- Purpose: Lets distance and access function as a progress clock.
- Failure if removed: Direction reversals or unrelated cuts make the chase geography incoherent.

### inv-03

- Rule: Hold successful boarding briefly as a false resolution before any destruction.
- Purpose: Gives the audience a concrete state to lose.
- Failure if removed: Immediate destruction reads as undifferentiated action rather than reversal.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use exactly one irreversible event to remove the route while withholding the protagonist's state.
- Purpose: Creates a clean information gap without repetitive spectacle.
- Failure if removed: Showing the protagonist safe during the event eliminates the reframe.

### inv-05

- Rule: Reintroduce the same adult at a separate safe vantage with three identity anchors and one held look back.
- Purpose: Proves survival and spatial separation while inviting reinterpretation.
- Failure if removed: A new identity or fleeting insert looks like a continuity error rather than a reveal.
