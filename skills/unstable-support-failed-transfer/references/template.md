# 动态承载｜平衡升级到失败落点 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one participant, a visible route and a stable traversal baseline before introducing a support surface that moves beneath the participant. Prove balance through several views that keep body, moving support and fixed landmarks together, then allow exactly one transfer attempt off the support. Close on the visible miss, impact medium and the participant's own reaction without inventing injury, score or victory.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one participant, route direction, hazard medium and destination before the hard section.
- Purpose: Makes success and failure spatially measurable.
- Failure if removed: Without route geography, later imbalance has no clear consequence.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show a stable traversal phase before the moving support.
- Purpose: Creates a competence baseline.
- Failure if removed: Starting directly on the hard obstacle removes escalation.

### inv-03

- Rule: Transfer the participant onto one support whose motion is visible against fixed landmarks.
- Purpose: Makes instability physical rather than performative.
- Failure if removed: A stationary prop cannot explain the balance corrections.

### inv-04

- Rule: Sustain balance long enough to prove repeated body correction while preserving support and destination in shared geography.
- Purpose: Builds credible tension before the exit.
- Failure if removed: Fragmentary close-ups hide whether the participant actually traverses.

### inv-05

- Rule: Use one terminal transfer attempt and show miss, impact medium and the participant's reaction without an invented outcome claim.
- Purpose: Turns failure into complete evidence rather than humiliation montage.
- Failure if removed: Omitting impact or reaction makes the ending ambiguous.
