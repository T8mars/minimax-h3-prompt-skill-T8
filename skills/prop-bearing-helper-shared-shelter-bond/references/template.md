# 道具携带者主动共享｜遮蔽物到位后完成亲密确认 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original waiting subject exposed to a clearly visible environmental pressure and a readable nearby shelter boundary. Introduce a second original subject carrying one active protective prop; let the prop enter early and keep its coverage edge visible as the helper closes distance. Complete the practical action first by placing both subjects inside one shared protected zone, then add exactly one restrained affiliative gesture such as a nuzzle, hand squeeze or shoulder lean. Hold the final shared shelter while the pressure remains visible outside. The reusable mechanism is exposed waiting state, prop-first helper entrance, coverage-as-progress, practical protection before affection and one-contact bond confirmation—not the source puppy, kitten, red umbrella, porch, rain, fur, exact poses, camera, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one waiting subject, one visible environmental pressure and an uncovered state before help arrives.
- Purpose: Makes the practical need immediately readable.
- Failure if removed: Without exposure, the protective prop has no function.

### inv-02

- Rule: Let the active protective prop enter before or with the helper and keep its coverage boundary visible.
- Purpose: Turns the solution into a visual promise and progress measure.
- Failure if removed: A hidden prop makes the approach semantically weak.

### inv-03

- Rule: Close distance on one legible axis until both subjects occupy the same protected zone.
- Purpose: Converts approach into a completed practical action.
- Failure if removed: Random cutting can conceal whether protection was actually shared.

### inv-04

- Rule: Complete protection before using an affiliative gesture.
- Purpose: Lets care arise from action rather than sentimental decoration.
- Failure if removed: Affection before shelter weakens the causal story.

### inv-05

- Rule: Use exactly one restrained contact and hold while pressure remains outside the cover.
- Purpose: Confirms the bond and proves the new state.
- Failure if removed: Repeated gestures or removing the pressure turns the ending into generic cuteness.
