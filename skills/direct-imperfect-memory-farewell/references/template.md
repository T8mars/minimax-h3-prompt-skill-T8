# Imperfect-Memory Farewell Take Template

## Mechanism

An imperfect continuous observer discovers a subject already engaged in an ordinary routine, lingers through small unperformed actions, then lets one external cue change the subject's goal. One late acknowledgment briefly closes the distance between subject and operator before a motivated exit and imperfect recording finish.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Begin with the operator discovering the subject inside legible everyday geography.
- Purpose: Establishes a lived observer relationship.
- Failure if removed: A centered hero opening feels staged and weakens the later acknowledgment.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give the subject at least two low-amplitude routine actions before the cue.
- Purpose: Builds personality and patience without exposition.
- Failure if removed: Immediate cue and exit produce a generic departure shot.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one environmental cue to cause a clear goal change in the same geography.
- Purpose: Creates narrative motion without a cut or speech.
- Failure if removed: The exit is arbitrary; multiple cues fragment attention.

### inv-04

- Rule: Reserve one brief acknowledgment for the threshold just before exit.
- Purpose: Turns detached observation into personal memory while keeping behavior candid.
- Failure if removed: Repeated posing feels performative; no acknowledgment wastes the observer relationship.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let imperfections follow operator attention without hiding the causal action.
- Purpose: Makes capture human while protecting readability.
- Failure if removed: Constant shake is only a filter; flawless coverage loses memory texture.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- Do not recreate the source transit setting, wardrobe, props, subject appearance, cultural signage, route, exact routine sequence, medium branding, autofocus targets, or ending.
- Change the place class, subject/operator relationship, routine objects, cue mechanics, capture format, acknowledgment, exit path, and operator mistake together.

## Transfer Test

1. Sketch the continuous geography and exit.
2. List at least two routine actions before the cue.
3. State exactly how the cue changes the goal.
4. Reserve one acknowledgment for the exit threshold.
5. Tie every defect to a specific operator correction.
6. Reject a concept that remains “same farewell at another stop.”

## Quality Repairs

- **Subject poses throughout.** Keep attention on routine until one acknowledgment at the exit.
- **Analog defects dominate.** Tie each defect to one operator adjustment and protect the cue.
- **Cue and exit are unrelated.** Show a visible goal change before departure.
- **The concept remains a transit reskin.** Change place, routine, cue, relationship, medium, and exit behavior together.

## Non-Binding Transfer Examples

- A pool-closing Hi8 clip discovers a lifeguard finishing routine tasks; the cover motor starts, he gives one small nod at the gate, and the camera overshoots.
- A VHS-C bakery closing video follows flour and bagging routines; a delivery bell changes the goal, one playful lens tap precedes an alley exit, and white balance drifts.
- A pocket-DV observatory clip follows equipment cleanup; the dome begins closing at dawn, one lens-cap salute precedes the stair exit, and focus remains on the empty telescope.
