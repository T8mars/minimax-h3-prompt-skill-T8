# 空场异常｜物质扩张与片名停留 Template

## Mechanism

An ordered empty location and one marked mundane object create a baseline. A single traceable material anomaly spreads in relation to that locus, then the camera holds the changed evidence under a distinct overlaid original title; the material is not assumed to generate the title or to have an unseen exact cause.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish an orderly, visibly unoccupied location and isolate one ordinary marked object within it.
- Purpose: Fixes the visual baseline and gives attention a concrete locus.
- Failure if removed: Many anomalies or shifting geography make the later change arbitrary.

### inv-02

- Rule: Introduce one connected material anomaly that grows in a stable area while the camera proves its relation to the marked object/location; do not assert an unseen cause.
- Purpose: Allows unease to escalate through visible evidence.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated jump scares or disconnected patches break the single-clue mechanism.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use short credits before the anomaly peaks; place a distinct original legible title as overlay on a held evidence composition.
- Purpose: Maintains title-sequence hierarchy without pretending the material spells the letters.
- Failure if removed: Illegible moving text or title appearing before evidence blunts the payoff.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| empty_location | Stable, orderly place with no visible person. |
| marked_object | One visually distinguishable object that attracts attention. |
| material_anomaly | One connected progressive change, with source not falsely asserted. |
| evidence_view | View that proves changed material relative to stable setting. |
| credits | Short invented readable overlays before final reveal. |
| title | Original legible text separate from physical anomaly. |
| sound | Original sparse sound arc, not attributed to unheard source. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 异常有起点
- 变化后证据持续存在
- 标题不被误写成异常产物

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- No pear, black bow, dinner table, orchard, wine-red tabletop spill or source title/credit names.
- No claim that the source liquid is blood or that the pear causes it.
- No exact table-tracking/plate-overhead composition transfer or source serif layout.

## Transfer Tests

1. In an original empty observatory, one abandoned paper star chart lies beneath a brass telescope; a small frost crystal appears on the eyepiece and spreads continuously along the connected brass tube and ring mount; a final overhead view holds the frosted instrument while the original title 'COLD MERIDIAN' overlays it.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03
   - Changed: empty_location, marked_object, material_anomaly, evidence_view, credits, title, sound
2. In an original deserted ferry terminal, one unclaimed blue luggage tag hangs from a bench; a single connected wet drag mark appears at its base and extends continuously across the same tiled concourse; a stable high view holds the trace and the original title 'LAST DEPARTURE' as an overlay.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03
   - Changed: empty_location, marked_object, material_anomaly, evidence_view, credits, title, sound
