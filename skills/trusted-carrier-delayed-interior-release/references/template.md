# 延迟载体释放｜进入—静置—开启—扩散 Template

## Mechanism

Show a sealed carrier being admitted through a visible external process, place it intact inside a trusted bounded space, preserve its identity through a readable delay or ambient-state change, let it visibly open only after entry with no opening operator shown, then let concealed contents emerge and create an environment-scale consequence. The source does not prove the hidden actuation method; the mechanism is independent of warfare, animals, cities, fire or paper craft.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Use a visible external process to admit one sealed carrier through a trusted boundary.
- Purpose: Makes entry causal rather than magical.
- Failure if removed: A carrier that simply appears inside has no admission proof.

### inv-02

- Rule: Establish the carrier intact and inert inside the bounded destination.
- Purpose: Creates trust and postpones the reveal.
- Failure if removed: Opening during entry removes the delayed-release premise.

### inv-03

- Rule: Preserve carrier identity and location through one readable delay or ambient-state change.
- Purpose: Separates admission from activation.
- Failure if removed: No delay makes the result an ordinary delivery.

### inv-04

- Rule: Open the carrier only after the delay, show no opening operator and let concealed contents emerge.
- Purpose: Reverses the trusted object's visible role without inventing an actuation cause.
- Failure if removed: Contents visible before the delayed opening remove the concealed-release turn.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let the released contents produce one visible environment-scale consequence.
- Purpose: Pays off the concealed capacity.
- Failure if removed: A local exit without a wider consequence leaves the carrier function unproven.
