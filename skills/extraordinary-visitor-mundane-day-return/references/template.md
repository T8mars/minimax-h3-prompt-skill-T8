# 异客日常化｜多项普通任务累积到黄昏返回 Template

## Mechanism

Open on one unmistakably extraordinary but original visitor at a familiar domestic threshold. Preserve the same identity anchors across a one-way daytime route of at least four visually different mundane responsibilities. Each chapter must contain one familiar tool or exchange, one complete action and one visible result; surrounding people and environments respond normally rather than explaining the visitor. Use object or hand-state handoffs to keep the montage causal, then change the time of day once and return the visitor to the opening boundary for a restrained final acknowledgment. The reusable mechanism is extraordinary identity normalized by cumulative ordinary competence and a same-place day return—not the source alien design, suburban house, car, service window, drink, grill, manicure, garden, clothing, exact tasks, camera positions, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one original extraordinary visitor at an ordinary threshold before the route begins.
- Purpose: Creates an immediate contrast question without requiring exposition.
- Failure if removed: A conventional subject removes the normalization tension; a random location removes the social baseline.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep the same identity anchors across at least four non-repeating mundane tasks.
- Purpose: Turns the premise into a coherent day rather than a novelty reel.
- Failure if removed: Identity drift or repeated duties break cumulative familiarity.

### inv-03

- Rule: Give every task one familiar tool or exchange, a complete action and a visible result.
- Purpose: Lets the audience understand competence without narration.
- Failure if removed: Poses or incomplete motions leave only costume spectacle.

### inv-04

- Rule: Let the world respond normally and avoid explaining the visitor's origin.
- Purpose: Normalization is produced through behavior and social acceptance.
- Failure if removed: Exposition or panic changes the mechanism into lore or threat.

### inv-05

- Rule: Use one day-to-night transition and return to the opening boundary with a restrained acknowledgment.
- Purpose: Converts multiple errands into a bounded daily loop.
- Failure if removed: A random final location leaves the route episodic and unresolved.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source gray alien head, eyes, body proportions, shirt, hands and exact greeting performance
- the source suburban porch, car interior, road, drive-through window, drink and payment exchange
- the source grill, meat, manicure interaction, garden hose, plants and residential night background
- the source shot order, lenses, framing, lighting progression, exact 30-second rhythm and transitions
- the source creator identity, platform overlay, watermark, model claim and branding
