# 路线揭幕｜重复通道依次亮出原创反应场景 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one recurring transit route as the connective carrier. Use a fully motivated moving occluder to replace that route with one self-contained original reaction tableau in a fixed reveal zone. Give each tableau a different adult cast, prop family and single readable relationship change, hold it briefly, then restore the same route before the next reveal. End on a completed original reaction rather than a partial wipe. The reusable mechanism is recurring-route continuity, motivated occlusion, distinct tableau revelation and reaction closure—not any source meme, recognizable likeness, exact pose, city block, taxi, vehicle interior, food gesture, wardrobe, timing, watermark, creator identity or model label.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one recurring route and one repeatable reveal zone before the first tableau.
- Purpose: Provides connective geography for otherwise separate situations.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated establishing shots turn the result into an ordinary compilation.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use a physically motivated object to create a complete visual occlusion before every reveal.
- Purpose: Makes each replacement attributable and hides the spatial handoff cleanly.
- Failure if removed: An arbitrary cut loses the route-based montage grammar.

### inv-03

- Rule: Give every tableau one distinct adult cast, prop family and readable relationship change.
- Purpose: Prevents the sequence from repeating the same joke with cosmetic swaps.
- Failure if removed: Copying familiar poses or reusing the same interaction becomes imitation rather than a reusable system.

### inv-04

- Rule: Return to the same route between tableaux while preserving direction and environmental anchors.
- Purpose: Keeps the montage legible as one relay rather than disconnected sketches.
- Failure if removed: Changing the street axis or travel direction removes connective continuity.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on a fully readable original reaction with no unfinished wipe.
- Purpose: Provides a terminal payoff and a countable final state.
- Failure if removed: Ending during transit leaves the last tableau unresolved.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- all recognizable source meme tableaux, public likenesses, facial identities, signature poses and reaction timing
- the source city block, storefronts, taxis, road vehicles, car interior and repeated urban route
- the source phone, cup, red-dress glance tableau, child passenger reaction, food-service gesture and yellow-shirt reaction
- the source wardrobe, prop colors, camera positions, transition cadence and exact scene order
- the source creator identity, Seedance 2.5 claim, platform overlays, watermark and any visible branding
