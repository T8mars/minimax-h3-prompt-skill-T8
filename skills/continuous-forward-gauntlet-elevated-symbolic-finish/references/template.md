# 连续前进闯阵｜防守闸门升级到高点标志 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult protagonist, one portable action tool, a visible forward objective axis and at least two recurring route landmarks. Use one continuous forward camera with no reveal cut while at least three defensive gates enter from different spatial directions. Require a different physical solution at each gate and hand the direction or momentum of one solution directly into the next forward movement. The protagonist may be slowed or displaced but must not reset to the starting state. After the final ground gate, reveal an already established vertical route, follow the protagonist upward and end with one original symbolic object placed at a high point, followed by a readable wide proof of the completed path. The reusable mechanism is uninterrupted forward geography, escalating defender gates, motion-to-motion handoff, ground-to-vertical release and symbolic route closure—not the source fighter, body, clothing, staff, soldiers, war, buildings, national flag, history, choreography, camera path, end card, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult protagonist, one portable action tool, a forward objective axis and at least two recurring route landmarks.
- Purpose: Creates a stable identity and geography for the continuous passage.
- Failure if removed: Without recurring landmarks, an apparent one-take can hide unrelated scene changes.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep one continuous forward camera with no reveal cut while at least three defensive gates enter from different spatial directions.
- Purpose: Makes progress and escalation spatially verifiable.
- Failure if removed: Cutting between gates breaks the route and can conceal resets.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use a different physical solution at each gate and hand its outgoing direction or momentum into the next advance.
- Purpose: Prevents repetitive combat and preserves causal flow.
- Failure if removed: Identical attacks separated by pauses read as a montage rather than one evolving run.

### inv-04

- Rule: Allow slowing or displacement but never return the protagonist to the starting state or erase accumulated route progress.
- Purpose: Preserves the cost and direction of the traversal.
- Failure if removed: A reset invalidates the continuous-objective promise.

### inv-05

- Rule: After the final ground gate, follow an already established vertical route and place one original symbolic object at a high point before a wide proof hold.
- Purpose: Converts physical passage into a visible terminal purpose.
- Failure if removed: A sudden roof cut or unexplained symbol turns the ending into detached iconography.
