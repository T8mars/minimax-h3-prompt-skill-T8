# 重击对轻群体反制｜诱饵让位、群体绕行与武器缠束 Template

## Mechanism

Establish two clearly adult original opponents, one heavy attack system and one light swarm system. Require the heavy attack to commit a readable path before the swarm changes formation. Make the swarm evade, preserve units and regroup behind the attack path to bind one functional point. Escalate binding through the attacker's repeated force while keeping both adult identities and geography readable. End on a stable immobilization proof with the controller unharmed and the attacker's original weapon still identifiable. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.
