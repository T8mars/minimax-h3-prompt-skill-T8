# 反差喜剧｜越从容越失控 Template

## Mechanism

Place an impeccably controlled subject inside a rigid public ritual, introduce one initially manageable physical liability, escalate its collateral effects in countable steps, then expose a visible failure while the subject preserves exactly the same confident performance rule. The mismatch between increasing chaos and unchanged poise creates the payoff; casting, setting, hazard, costume, palette and final consequence are variable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish a formal ritual with a clear success posture before disruption begins.
- Purpose: Gives the audience a behavioral baseline.
- Failure if removed: Without a stable ritual, later chaos reads as ordinary disorder.

### inv-02

- Rule: Introduce one physical liability attached to the subject and keep its cause visible.
- Purpose: Makes each collision causally legible.
- Failure if removed: Random background accidents break the comedy engine.

### inv-03

- Rule: Escalate collateral consequences at least twice while the subject maintains the same poise.
- Purpose: Builds a countable deadpan ladder.
- Failure if removed: Changing attitude too early releases tension before the payoff.

### inv-04

- Rule: Show one irreversible visible failure only after collateral has peaked.
- Purpose: Converts disruption into a clean state change.
- Failure if removed: An early failure removes the escalation ladder.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on confident continuation rather than explanation or embarrassment.
- Purpose: Turns failure into a controlled mic-drop.
- Failure if removed: Apology or panic replaces deadpan comedy with ordinary mishap.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original subject whose identity and silhouette remain stable. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| escalation_variable | One readable variable that changes monotonically or in countable stages. |
| performance_rule | One restrained performance rule that makes the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | A coherent information order that does not copy the source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | One clean physical or graphic consequence that closes the causal chain. |
| sound_family | A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting each state change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 正式场合与沉着主体
- 负担始终附着主体
- 至少两次因果连锁
- 失效可见但主体不慌

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- upright sunglasses-wearing reptile on a luxury runway
- iridescent white feather train sweeping seated spectators
- black curtain entrance and reflective fashion catwalk
- source pivot, collision positions and stripped-cape ending

## Transfer Tests

1. A poised pastry chef presents a towering kinetic sugar sculpture on a live studio turntable; each elegant adjustment triggers a larger set failure, the outer sugar sail snaps, and she completes the bow without breaking her smile.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A ceremonial museum guide unveils a wind-driven paper installation whose ribbons progressively topple display stanchions; one panel detaches, yet the guide completes the scripted gesture with perfect calm.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
