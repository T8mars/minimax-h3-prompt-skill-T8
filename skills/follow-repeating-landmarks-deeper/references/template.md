# 连续推进｜重复地标计时并抵达更深通道 Template

## Mechanism

Move continuously through one constrained passage, use repeated fixed landmarks and doorway parallax as a progress clock, then turn or cross once at a late visible threshold so the endpoint is a materially deeper secondary passage without inventing an inspection, diagnosis or unseen threat.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Use one continuous constrained passage and stable travel axis.
- Purpose: Makes progress spatially auditable.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated corridors erase route continuity.

### inv-02

- Rule: Repeat fixed landmarks as a progress clock.
- Purpose: Proves forward motion in low visibility.
- Failure if removed: Featureless darkness cannot show distance.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep moving while one late doorway grows through parallax.
- Purpose: Turns approach into visible progress rather than an invented inspection.
- Failure if removed: A sudden unrelated doorway cut breaks route continuity.

### inv-04

- Rule: Turn or cross only once at the late visible threshold.
- Purpose: Creates one decisive spatial handoff.
- Failure if removed: Repeated detours turn the passage into arbitrary exploration.

### inv-05

- Rule: End inside a materially deeper secondary passage while retaining one prior-route edge.
- Purpose: Proves arrival and preserves orientation.
- Failure if removed: Stopping before the threshold or hiding all prior geometry feels truncated.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| causal_carrier | One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain. |
| state_change | Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Use one continuous constrained passage and stable travel axis.
- Repeat fixed landmarks as a progress clock.
- Turn or cross only once at the late visible threshold.
- End inside a materially deeper secondary passage while retaining one prior-route edge.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source dark subway tunnel and rail corridor
- source first-person game-like viewpoint
- the exact doors, lamps and tunnel route
- any unobserved creature or threat claim

## Transfer Tests

1. A botanical maintenance camera moves continuously through a root-observation trench, counts identical moisture windows, then turns once through a late circular airlock into a darker seed-vault corridor while the last window remains at frame edge.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. An overhead kiln camera follows a circular service ring past repeating heat shields; one radial door grows larger through parallax, then the camera crosses into a cool rectangular glaze tunnel with the ring edge still visible behind.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
