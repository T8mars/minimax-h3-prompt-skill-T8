# 手势边界换媒｜局部窗口、多形态验真与回归 Template

## Mechanism

A stable live-action baseline becomes an interactive proof surface: two visible hands first define a bounded aperture, only the registered area inside that moving geometry switches medium, repeated size and orientation changes prove the rule, a late large-coverage state supplies the apex, and a controlled dismissal restores the baseline for one confirming reaction or residue.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult original subject, one stable environment and visibly empty or inactive hands before the boundary activates.
- Failure if removed: If the baseline is missing, viewers cannot distinguish the transformation from an already-stylized full-frame filter.

### inv-02

- Rule: Make the two-hand geometry visibly form before the medium switch, and keep the luminous or material boundary attached to the controlling fingertips or hand edges.
- Failure if removed: If the region appears independently of the hands, the central cause-and-control relationship collapses.

### inv-03

- Rule: Confine the alternate medium strictly inside the moving boundary while preserving continuous registration across the edge and leaving the exterior in its baseline state.
- Failure if removed: If conversion leaks outside or registration slides, the aperture stops reading as a controllable local window.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use at least three materially different aperture geometries or orientations, with each state revealing a new region while obeying the same clipping rule.
- Failure if removed: One static rectangle is only a filter demo; repeated rule-consistent geometry is what proves reusable control.

### inv-05

- Rule: Reserve the largest or most identity-sensitive coverage for a late apex, then visibly contract or dismiss the boundary instead of allowing an uncontrolled full-scene takeover.
- Failure if removed: Without a late apex and controlled release, the sequence lacks escalation and the ending state becomes ambiguous.

### inv-06

- Rule: End in the original medium and spatial registration with one short subject reaction or physical residue that confirms the effect has ended.
- Failure if removed: If the film ends inside the alternate medium, control and reversibility are not proven.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| adult_subject | One clearly adult original controller with three stable appearance anchors and readable hands. |
| base_environment | A stable, simply registered space whose lines and lighting make local conversion easy to verify. |
| boundary_material | A luminous, drawn, mechanical or material edge visibly attached to both hands. |
| alternate_medium | A medium with clearly different rendering physics, such as scientific linework, paper blueprint, stained glass or clay relief. |
| proof_regions | At least three differently shaped or oriented local regions that all obey the same clipping and registration rule. |
| apex_region | The largest late-stage coverage, ideally crossing an identity-sensitive or information-rich area. |
| ending_confirmation | One restrained reaction or physical residue after the boundary is dismissed. |
| sound_family | Original edge-drawing, stretch, lock, medium-shift and release cues; semantic source audio is not reused. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- 激活前先建立一名明确成年原创控制者、稳定环境和可见的空闲双手。
- 双手几何必须先于换媒形成，活动边缘始终绑定指尖或手部边线。
- 替代媒介严格限制在移动边界内部，边界两侧空间精确注册，外部保持基线状态。
- 至少用三种几何或覆盖区证明同一规则；最大覆盖留到后段，再主动收回并以原媒介反应或残留确认结束。

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- Do not reproduce any source-specific performer identity, appearance, wardrobe or proportions.
- Do not reuse the source environment, boundary styling, alternate-medium character design or exact transformation surface.
- Do not copy the precise hand poses, geometry order, timing, reactions, framing or soundtrack.
- Do not claim any source prompt, creator identity or generating model; those facts were not supplied.
