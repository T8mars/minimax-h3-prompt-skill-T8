# 实用整理确认关系｜接触、退开与双向微反应 Template

## Mechanism

Establish two original clearly adult roles in one quiet space, one visible garment or equipment irregularity and one stable shared camera axis. Let the helper perform exactly one continuous, functionally legible adjustment with both hands while the receiving adult remains responsive but restrained. Show the adjusted item reaching a measurable finished state before the helper pauses and withdraws. Give each adult one distinct microreaction after contact ends, without relying on unverified dialogue semantics, then return to a shared composition where the finished item and both stable postures prove readiness. The reusable mechanism is practical contact, continuous adjustment, completion pause, separation, reciprocal microreaction and nonverbal readiness confirmation—not the source named characters, faces, hair, uniforms, emblems, neckwear color, franchise, military setting, exact expressions, dialogue, composition, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly adult original roles, one visible irregularity and one stable shared camera axis.
- Purpose: Makes the practical need and relationship geometry legible before contact.
- Failure if removed: Without a visible issue, touching can read as arbitrary or purely romantic.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use exactly one continuous, functionally clear two-hand adjustment.
- Purpose: Concentrates attention on a verifiable act of care and preparation.
- Failure if removed: Multiple unrelated touches dilute the causal action.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show a measurable finished state before the helper pauses and withdraws.
- Purpose: Proves the task succeeded and creates a transition from action to emotion.
- Failure if removed: Withdrawing before completion leaves both readiness and motivation unresolved.

### inv-04

- Rule: Give both adults different restrained microreactions only after contact ends.
- Purpose: Lets reciprocity emerge from the completed action rather than overt exposition.
- Failure if removed: Large simultaneous reactions overpower the quiet relational mechanism.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return to a shared composition where the finished item and stable postures remain visible.
- Purpose: Closes practical and relational evidence together.
- Failure if removed: Ending on an isolated face can hide whether the adjustment persisted.
