# 目标先现的仪式行进｜前景队列与地标尺度同步放大 Template

## Mechanism

Reveal one original monumental destination in full at the opening together with a clearly readable approach route and at least three clearly adult travelers or carriers in the foreground. Preserve one objective direction and one stable destination identity for the entire shot. Use repeated group anchors such as flags, garments, lights or carrier forms to turn ordinary forward movement into a processional rhythm. Combine traveler motion with one restrained camera push and slight elevation change so the destination gains architectural or environmental scale layers monotonically while foreground figures reduce. End on a held destination-dominant wide that retains at least two small traveler or route anchors, proving continued relationship rather than replacing the journey with a disconnected postcard. The reusable mechanism is destination-first promise, processional repetition, continuous approach, monotonic landmark scale gain and traveler-retaining terminal grandeur—not the source Camelot premise, knights, horses, heraldry, castle, valley, flags, route, sunset palette, exact composition, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Show one monumental destination, one readable route and at least three clearly adult travelers or carriers together in the opening.
- Purpose: Creates a destination-first promise with measurable scale and direction.
- Failure if removed: Hiding the destination changes the mechanism into a reveal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Preserve one objective direction and one stable destination identity throughout.
- Purpose: Makes all motion contribute to the same approach.
- Failure if removed: Direction reversals or landmark substitution break journey continuity.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one repeated group anchor to create processional rhythm across the moving foreground.
- Purpose: Turns generic travel into a collective ceremonial action.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated travelers read as traffic rather than a procession.

### inv-04

- Rule: Combine forward travel with a restrained push and slight elevation change so destination scale layers increase monotonically.
- Purpose: Builds grandeur without cuts, transformations or surprise events.
- Failure if removed: Aggressive orbiting or scale regression makes the geography unstable.

### inv-05

- Rule: Hold a destination-dominant final view while retaining at least two traveler or route anchors.
- Purpose: Proves the journey relationship survives the scale payoff.
- Failure if removed: Removing every traveler turns the ending into a disconnected landscape image.
