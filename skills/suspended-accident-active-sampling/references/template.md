# 事故悬停｜主动取样后独自离场 Template

## Mechanism

Establish an ordinary shared routine, interrupt it with one physical accident that distributes several readable objects, and suspend the accident at maximum disorder. Let exactly one subject retain normal agency, deliberately sample at least two separated pieces of the frozen event, then leave while the wider tableau remains visibly locked. The persistent world state, not a later restart, is the payoff.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish an ordinary routine and the routes of at least two participants before the accident.
- Purpose: Makes the interruption causally readable.
- Failure if removed: Opening on suspended debris turns the event into unexplained decoration.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one accident to distribute several independently readable objects across depth.
- Purpose: Creates a testable stopped-world field.
- Failure if removed: One floating prop cannot prove a shared physical state.

### inv-03

- Rule: At peak disorder, keep all participants and objects fixed except one clearly mobile subject.
- Purpose: Gives the anomaly an unambiguous agency rule.
- Failure if removed: If several people move, the freeze becomes an editing ambiguity.

### inv-04

- Rule: Have the mobile subject selectively remove at least two separated objects without releasing the rest.
- Purpose: Proves deliberate interaction inside the fixed state.
- Failure if removed: A passive walk-through never tests the suspension.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with the subject leaving while a wider view still proves the accident remains suspended.
- Purpose: Turns persistence into the final consequence.
- Failure if removed: A global restart would replace the mechanism with a generic time-freeze release.
