# 语义字效｜短语物理逐级升级到整句锁定 Template

## Mechanism

Split one original sentence into ordered semantic phrases. Give each phrase one legible typographic state and one physical operation that expresses its meaning, while preserving a visible carrier from the previous phase. Increase contrast, spatial scale and motion energy in controlled steps, then reverse the priority at the end: stop all movement, restore the full sentence and hold it long enough to read. The reusable mechanism is semantic phrase-to-physics escalation followed by complete-language lockup, not the source quotation, fonts, palette or exact effects.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Begin with one original sentence divided into a fixed semantic order; reveal the first phrase from a minimal baseline.
- Purpose: Creates a language-first causal spine.
- Failure if removed: A decorative opening without ordered text gives later effects nothing to develop.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give every new phrase one readable typographic state and one physical behavior tied to its semantic role.
- Purpose: Makes meaning drive motion instead of decorating it.
- Failure if removed: Generic transitions or illegible words sever the phrase-to-effect relationship.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry one visible element, material or motion consequence from each phase into the next.
- Purpose: Maintains one continuous argument across changing designs.
- Failure if removed: Independent title cards become an unrelated typography reel.

### inv-04

- Rule: Escalate at least two of scale, contrast, spatial depth or motion energy in a controlled sequence.
- Purpose: Builds retention and makes the terminal maximum earned.
- Failure if removed: Equal-intensity phases flatten the arc; simultaneous excess becomes chaotic.

### inv-05

- Rule: Stop the escalation and reassemble the complete sentence in a stable readable final hold.
- Purpose: Converts spectacle back into comprehension.
- Failure if removed: Ending on fragments or one giant word leaves the language unresolved.

- the source MiniMax and Hailuo marks

- the source exact timing, camera pass-through and three red emphasized words in the final card
