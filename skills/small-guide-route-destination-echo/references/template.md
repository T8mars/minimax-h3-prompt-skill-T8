# 微型引路｜重复现身到终点材质回声 Template

## Mechanism

Introduce one small nonverbal guide, let it repeatedly appear at spatially connected markers while a follower or camera advances, and end at a destination whose material, shape or color visibly echoes the guide's established identity.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one small moving guide and its scale relation before the destination is known.
- Purpose: Creates a legible attention hook.
- Failure if removed: A destination-first opening removes the guide's purpose.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep guide, follower and route direction spatially connected during movement.
- Purpose: Makes the journey continuous rather than a montage.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated locations turn reappearances into teleportation.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use at least two recurring apertures or landmarks as route-progress proofs.
- Purpose: Makes repeated guide sightings cumulative.
- Failure if removed: One appearance cannot demonstrate guidance.

### inv-04

- Rule: Let the guide enter or indicate the final destination before the follower arrives.
- Purpose: Converts motion into an earned endpoint.
- Failure if removed: A follower arriving first makes the guide irrelevant.

### inv-05

- Rule: Echo one established guide property in the destination result.
- Purpose: Pays off the guide's visual identity without dialogue.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated endpoint makes the route arbitrary.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Establish one small moving guide and its scale relation before the destination is known.
- Keep guide, follower and route direction spatially connected during movement.
- Let the guide enter or indicate the final destination before the follower arrives.
- Echo one established guide property in the destination result.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source bee
- market visitor and market stalls
- the source shop apertures
- honey pastry or food display
- the exact bee-follow route and warm food palette

## Transfer Tests

1. A glowing paper moth guides a junior archivist through circular shelf windows to a reading alcove where its wing pattern repeats in a newly illuminated map.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A tiny blue maintenance fish leads an inspection camera through connected coral service rings to a filtration chamber whose membrane pulses with the same blue stripe pattern.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
