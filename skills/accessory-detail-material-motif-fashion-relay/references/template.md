# 配饰材质接力｜细节证据、全身回连与原创品牌收束 Template

## Mechanism

Lock one original clearly adult model with at least three identity anchors and one original material motif. Move through at least four different accessory classes. In each module, show one tactile operation or structural proof before allowing the same highlight, texture, edge geometry or sound to match into the next object. After the detail ladder, reconnect all objects to the same adult's full-body posture and gait. End on one completely original short brand name with no source marks or product silhouettes. The reusable mechanism is tactile accessory evidence, material-motif relay, scale expansion, full-body reconnection and original identity closure—not the source model, luxury brand, monogram, product designs, white set, exact shots, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original model and one original material motif before product close-ups.
- Purpose: Provides human and visual continuity.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated hands and products feel like a catalog collage.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use at least four different accessory classes and one tactile structural proof per class.
- Purpose: Demonstrates breadth and function.
- Failure if removed: Beauty-only close-ups lack evidence of object behavior.

### inv-03

- Rule: Match the same highlight, texture, edge or sound from each item into the next.
- Purpose: Creates a causal visual relay.
- Failure if removed: Hard cuts between unrelated objects lose campaign coherence.

### inv-04

- Rule: Reconnect detail modules to the same adult's full-body posture and gait.
- Purpose: Shows how objects form one look.
- Failure if removed: Ending on isolated products loses the wearer.

### inv-05

- Rule: Finish with a completely original short brand name and no source product silhouette or mark.
- Purpose: Creates distributable identity closure.
- Failure if removed: Source branding or lookalike shapes violate anti-copy boundaries.
