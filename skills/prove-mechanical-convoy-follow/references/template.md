# 机械同行｜启动后稳定伴行 Template

## Mechanism

Embed one impossible following behavior inside a familiar transport routine, make a visible device produce a clear causal threshold before the behavior begins, then sustain a single view long enough to prove a stable matched-speed convoy. Setup, activation, acceleration, lift and proof must remain continuous; operator, payload, lead transport, lift mechanism, route, camera axis and tone are variable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish the secondary payload grounded in an ordinary transport routine.
- Purpose: Creates a physical baseline.
- Failure if removed: Starting airborne makes the behavior arbitrary.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show one mechanical device fitted and activated before lift.
- Purpose: Provides a readable cause.
- Failure if removed: Hidden or late cause turns flight into unexplained magic.

### inv-03

- Rule: Let device output increase until a visible threshold triggers smooth lift.
- Purpose: Makes the transition mechanically legible.
- Failure if removed: Instant teleporting or jumping breaks causal continuity.

### inv-04

- Rule: Match the payload's forward speed to the lead transport in one sustained view.
- Purpose: Proves stable convoy behavior under motion.
- Failure if removed: A short cutaway cannot verify relative position.

### inv-05

- Rule: Keep the operator calm and the moving payload mechanically stable after the impossible event.
- Purpose: Preserves the deadpan whimsical tone without depending on a living companion.
- Failure if removed: Panic, wobble or loss of payload control reframes the proof as danger or failure.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original subject whose identity and silhouette remain stable. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| escalation_variable | One readable variable that changes monotonically or in countable stages. |
| performance_rule | One restrained performance rule that makes the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | A coherent information order that does not copy the source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | One clean physical or graphic consequence that closes the causal chain. |
| sound_family | A new hierarchy of diegetic cues supporting each state change. |

## Transfer Tests

The porter remains calm and the sealed crate stays rigid without wobble throughout the convoy proof.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
