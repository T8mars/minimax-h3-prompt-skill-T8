# 日常任务到呼吸复位｜前景过渡、静坐微动与继续离场 Template

## Mechanism

Lock one original clearly adult subject with at least three identity anchors. Begin with one ordinary tactile task and show its completion. Use one natural foreground object, doorway or architectural edge to transition into a separate rest geometry. Hold long enough to show exactly one slow breath and at least two micro-responses in hair, clothing, plants or light while the camera performs only a restrained push. After the reset, let the same adult resume movement along an established direction and cross one visible exit threshold. The reusable mechanism is task completion, natural wipe, measured breathing reset, environmental micro-response and resumed exit—not the source woman, sheet, bicycle, bench, ferns, gate, clothing, exact camera, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original subject and complete one ordinary tactile task before rest.
- Purpose: Gives stillness a before-state and purpose.
- Failure if removed: Opening directly on a bench becomes a generic portrait.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one natural foreground or architectural edge to transition into a distinct rest geometry.
- Purpose: Connects active and quiet states without arbitrary cutting.
- Failure if removed: A random dissolve loses spatial intention.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show exactly one slow breath and at least two micro-responses in body or environment.
- Purpose: Makes internal reset visible without melodrama.
- Failure if removed: No micro-response looks frozen; many gestures overplay the moment.

### inv-04

- Rule: Restrict camera movement during the breath to one slow push or hold.
- Purpose: Protects attention and temporal contrast.
- Failure if removed: Fast coverage destroys the reset.

### inv-05

- Rule: After rest, resume the same adult's movement and cross a visible exit threshold.
- Purpose: Proves renewal rather than terminal stillness.
- Failure if removed: Ending on the bench leaves no change in agency.
