# 连续目标防御｜固定核心、双角色接力与威胁环升级 Template

## Mechanism

Choose one original objective with a unique light or material signature and keep its location recoverable throughout one continuous camera path. Use two clearly different adult defender roles: one mobile precision role and one heavy space-control role. Transfer foreground priority at least three times through matched direction, occlusion or direct action consequence; track at least one projectile or tool effect from launch to impact. Escalate threat topology from a readable outer ring to close intrusion and then to an environmental state change. End with both defenders regrouped at the objective and show whether the objective remains stable, changes or fails. The reusable mechanism is fixed-objective continuous geography, role-swap causality and threat-ring escalation—not the source crystal, characters, creatures, weapons, armor, exact actions, camera route, style, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Keep one objective's position and visual signature recoverable through the entire continuous path.
- Purpose: Prevents a long action take from losing its strategic center.
- Failure if removed: If the objective disappears without spatial cues, the defense becomes generic combat.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one mobile precision defender and one heavy space-control defender with distinct functions.
- Purpose: Allows meaningful role swaps instead of interchangeable action bodies.
- Failure if removed: Identical roles make foreground transfers decorative.

### inv-03

- Rule: Transfer attention at least three times through matched direction, occlusion or direct action consequence.
- Purpose: Maintains causal continuity without hard cuts.
- Failure if removed: Arbitrary camera swings break the single-event chain.

### inv-04

- Rule: Track at least one projectile or tool effect from launch to visible impact.
- Purpose: Provides a verifiable action-result bridge inside the moving shot.
- Failure if removed: Impacts without launches or launches without outcomes weaken causality.

### inv-05

- Rule: Escalate the threat ring and finish with both roles plus the objective state visible.
- Purpose: Closes topology, teamwork and consequence together.
- Failure if removed: Ending on a random attacker or empty effect leaves the defense unresolved.
