# 阻断改道｜资源检查后落入替代活动 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one ordinary intended action and the environmental condition required to complete it. Introduce one visible external blocker, then show the character checking the specific enabling resource that could overcome it. Confirm that the resource is unavailable through legible physical evidence. Let the character choose a setting-compatible alternative activity, preserve the blocker in the background, and end on a stable affective proof that the revised plan is genuinely accepted. The reusable mechanism is intended action, external blocker, resource check, confirmed deficit, chosen alternative and settled payoff—not the source woman, bookstore, rain, umbrella, coffee, book, clothing, window layout, exact performance, creator identity or model claim.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one intended action and its enabling environmental condition before the blocker appears.
- Purpose: Gives the interruption a measurable goal cost.
- Failure if removed: Without a prior intention, the obstacle is only atmosphere.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep one external blocker visibly active while the character evaluates it.
- Purpose: Maintains causal pressure across the decision.
- Failure if removed: Removing the blocker before the choice makes the pivot unmotivated.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show a physical check for one specific resource that could overcome the blocker.
- Purpose: Turns decision logic into observable action.
- Failure if removed: A purely facial reaction hides the causal test.

### inv-04

- Rule: Confirm resource absence before beginning a setting-compatible alternative activity.
- Purpose: Separates failed solution from deliberate replanning.
- Failure if removed: Starting the alternative too early makes it look preplanned.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on stable emotional or behavioral evidence of acceptance while the blocker remains unresolved.
- Purpose: Proves adaptation rather than temporary distraction.
- Failure if removed: Reopening the original goal prevents closure.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source woman's face, hair, grey hoodie, bag and exact performance
- the source bookstore, cafe counter, shelves, rainy street, window geometry and warm-cool lighting arrangement
- the source umbrella check, coffee, book, table actions and exact event order or timing
- the source framing, depth of field, weather texture, expression sequence and character identity
- the source creator identity, Seedance 2.0 claim, platform overlay, watermark and branding

## Transfer Tests

1. A field researcher prepares to launch a sensor boat, but sudden lightning closes the lake; she checks the grounded battery shelter, finds it unpowered, then stays in the mobile lab to annotate samples and ends absorbed in the new task while lightning remains beyond the window.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A night baker plans a bicycle delivery, but a street parade blocks the route; he checks a folded permit map, finds no crossing, then turns the undelivered pastries into a small counter tasting and settles into serving nearby neighbors while the parade continues outside.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
