# 多人商品一致性｜常驻参考板与角色接力实证 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original product and at least three clearly adult characters in a persistent registered reference board that remains visible while the main image changes. Give every adult at least three stable identity anchors and give the product stable package geometry, proportion, color allocation and mark position. In the main image, relay attention between solo presentation, coordinated group movement, one visible use action, one material or result macro and a final group payoff; after every scene or character change, the persistent board must allow immediate visual comparison. End by handing the group composition to a clean isolated product lockup. The reusable mechanism is a constant visual audit baseline, multi-character role relay, product continuity across environments, material proof and group-to-pack closure—not the source people, faces, hair, clothing, snack, package text, corridor, garden, food layers, palette, layouts, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Register one persistent reference board containing the original product and at least three clearly adult character identities before the first scene change.
- Purpose: Creates a fixed audit baseline for every later character and product appearance.
- Failure if removed: Without a simultaneous baseline, continuity claims depend on memory and cannot be visually verified.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give each adult at least three stable identity anchors and give the product stable geometry, proportion, color allocation and mark position.
- Purpose: Separates intentional role changes from identity or package drift.
- Failure if removed: A shared wardrobe color alone cannot prove who or what remained consistent.

### inv-03

- Rule: Relay the main image through solo presentation, coordinated group movement and one visible product-use action while keeping the board registered.
- Purpose: Tests identity and package continuity under materially different staging duties.
- Failure if removed: Repeated static portraits do not prove consistency during motion or interaction.

### inv-04

- Rule: Insert one macro material or result proof that is causally linked to the same product.
- Purpose: Explains what the product delivers rather than offering only character spectacle.
- Failure if removed: A decorative macro with no package handoff breaks the commercial evidence chain.

### inv-05

- Rule: Reunite all adults before handing off to one clean isolated product lockup and readable hold.
- Purpose: Closes both the ensemble and product identity promises.
- Failure if removed: Ending on another character insert leaves the advertised object unresolved.
