# 刻意遮断省略｜动作升级后以审查卡跳到余波结果 Template

## Mechanism

Establish two original clearly adult-scale opposing figures and one stable confrontation geography. Escalate through at least three fully visible action beats, then at the maximum threshold deliberately replace the picture with an unmistakably authored original interruption card or graphic blackout. Do not depict, describe or infer the missing action. During the ellipse, use only restrained temporal evidence such as distant sound, rain or a counter. Return to the same geography and reveal only observable aftermath states of the same figures and environment. The reusable mechanism is legible buildup, explicit authored censorship-as-ellipsis, non-invention and aftermath reveal—not the source fighters, armor, alley, rain palette, CENSORED word, choreography, hidden content, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly adult-scale original figures and stable geography before escalation.
- Purpose: Makes pre/post comparison possible.
- Failure if removed: An undefined space prevents aftermath verification.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show at least three complete visible action beats before the interruption.
- Purpose: Earns the narrative threshold.
- Failure if removed: An early blackout feels like missing media rather than intentional form.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use an unmistakably authored original interruption card at the maximum threshold.
- Purpose: Labels absence as deliberate ellipsis.
- Failure if removed: A corrupt-looking cut can be mistaken for decode failure.

### inv-04

- Rule: Never depict, narrate or infer hidden action during the interruption.
- Purpose: Preserves evidence honesty and audience imagination.
- Failure if removed: Invented combat falsely claims source support.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return to the same geography and reveal only observable aftermath states.
- Purpose: Closes the comparison without solving the missing interval.
- Failure if removed: A new location or explanatory flashback breaks the ellipse.
