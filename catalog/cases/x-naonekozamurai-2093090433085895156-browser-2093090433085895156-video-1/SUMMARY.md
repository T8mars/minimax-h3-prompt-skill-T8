# 运动鞋功能证据｜运动承诺、微距材质与产品英雄收束

## 用途

先用运动动作提出功能问题，再以鞋底、鞋面和结构微距逐项证明；保持黑白青统一注册，最终让功能证据回到完整产品与行动号召。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one original product through macro material anchors before showing the complete object. + Connect at least two visible ingredient or design details to one coherent environment identity. + Use one functional product action as the sole optical transition into the environment. + Expand the environment, then enclose its recognizable geometry inside one transparent carrier. + Return the carrier and environment to the same complete product with stable shape, label-free identity and hero proof.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 成年夜班递送员在炭黑仓库地面冲刺，反光包裹带建立使用情境。；三次微距依次证明鞋底抓地、密封接缝与脚踝支撑。；完整产品与准确原创短词【READY】并置，停留完成证明。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- Performance context precedes the product reveal.
- The complete product profile remains recognizable.
- The user action visibly exercises those functions.
- The final hero frame reunites product, proof and exact short copy.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
