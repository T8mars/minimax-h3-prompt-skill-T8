# 时装板式轮播｜同一成年模特、服装章节与主标回归

## 用途

先锁定同一成年模特，再让不同造型在统一红黑白网格、编号和字块中逐章轮转；版式节拍替代随机换景，终局以同一人物和总标题完成身份回扣。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one clearly adult original fashion subject with at least three stable identity anchors. + Rotate distinct style modules while changing only declared wardrobe, accessory, palette and typography variables. + Bind every module to subject action, camera movement and foreground motion while preserving identity. + Insert one authored low-density pause after the maximum-intensity phase. + Return prior modules as a compressed recall and finish on the same adult in one stable hero frame.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 建立原创成年模特：银色短发，置于海军蓝、米白与珊瑚红编辑网格中，主标题为【FORM INDEX】。；用一张近景肖像与一张全身姿态交替，始终保留同一网格坐标和章节编号。；回到银发模特的稳定英雄姿态，并在准确标题【FORM INDEX / 05】下定格。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- One clearly adult identity persists across every outfit and crop.
- Each outfit occupies a discrete graphic chapter with no mid-body morphing.
- Close and full views alternate without changing the subject's anatomy.
- The master title and identity return together in the terminal lockup.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
