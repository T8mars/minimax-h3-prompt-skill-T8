# 粉黑动作字场变体｜成年奔跑者以行动生成空间短词

## 用途

把来源的青少年与刺客表面完全排除，改用原创明确成年人；角色在固定粉黑城市轴线上奔跑、落地、挥击和停步，每次动作开始后只生成一个语义短词，并让文字成为可落脚、可击中或可穿越的空间结构。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one clearly adult original subject, three identity anchors and one continuous world axis. + Bind each decisive action to exactly one short original semantic word that appears only after the action begins. + Make each word occupy, divide, reflect or redirect the physical environment rather than float as a subtitle. + Escalate typography scale and spatial depth while preserving identity, travel direction and readable action. + Return to the same adult face or full-body anchor and resolve the word sequence into one terminal identity proof.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 奶油与钴蓝图形城市上方建立原创明确成年跑酷制图师：铜色马尾、白色护目镜、炭灰连体服和青色肘带；她第一次踏上屋顶后才生成空间准确短词【ROUTE】。；落地击裂一块已注册网格地砖，并从冲击点升起实体准确短词【GROUND】；全部身份锚点保持稳定。；终局让成年人直立在坡道后，准确金属主标【MAP THE MOVE】同框；原始城市与前面两个路线锚点继续可见。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- One clearly adult original performer and a stable world axis are established before any semantic word appears.
- Each decisive action begins before exactly one related short word becomes visible.
- Identity and geography remain continuous across run, landing, strike and terminal stance.
- The final mark follows the completed route and retains at least two previously established spatial anchors.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
