# 材质字构穿行｜同一成年人物跨九类物质语义空间

## 用途

让同一明确成年人物穿过由烟、铬、植物、电流、阴影、纸张与丝带构成的语义空间；每种材质只承担一个短词和一个物理行为，最终汇入单一身份结论。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one clearly adult original subject and a fixed dark spatial axis before material typography appears. + Assign each module one short original word, one material family and one physical behavior. + Keep the subject identity and travel direction continuous while material physics change. + Compress prior modules through a bounded aperture, ribbon or paper relay rather than random cuts. + Converge all material channels into one stable terminal portrait and readable original title.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 黑色画廊内，一名明确成年栗发建筑师穿深灰长外套沿固定左右路径出现；她身后准确短词【FORM】由磨砂玻璃构成。；同一成年人进入由细琥珀光束构成的【LIGHT】和由悬挂象牙线绳构成的【THREAD】；脸、外套和移动方向稳定。；所有碎片沉降成原创编辑肖像；同一成年人面对镜头，旁边只显示准确标题【BUILT BY HAND】，完全停住。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- Establish one clearly adult original subject and a fixed dark spatial axis before material typography appears.
- Assign each module one short original word, one material family and one physical behavior.
- Compress prior modules through a bounded aperture, ribbon or paper relay rather than random cuts.
- Converge all material channels into one stable terminal portrait and readable original title.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
