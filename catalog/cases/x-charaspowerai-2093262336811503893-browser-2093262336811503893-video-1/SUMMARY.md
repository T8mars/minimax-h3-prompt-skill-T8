# 运动鞋使用证据｜涉水步态、路面压力与产品英雄镜头

## 用途

先让明确成年使用者在湿滑路线中提出需求，再用鞋底、鞋面和落地压力的微距逐项证明；完整产品始终可辨，最终回到单只与成对产品英雄镜头。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one original product through macro material anchors before showing the complete object. + Connect at least two visible ingredient or design details to one coherent environment identity. + Use one functional product action as the sole optical transition into the environment. + Expand the environment, then enclose its recognizable geometry inside one transparent carrier. + Return the carrier and environment to the same complete product with stable shape, label-free identity and hero proof.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 成年夜班急救员穿原创白蓝救援靴，穿过雨湿仓库地面。；三次微距依次证明排水纹路、密封接缝和受控鞋底压缩。；成对产品与湿地路线同场停留，三个功能证据同时可辨。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- Use context establishes the product need before macros.
- One product geometry remains recognizable throughout.
- Adult movement visibly exercises those functions.
- The final hero frame reunites product, route and proof.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
