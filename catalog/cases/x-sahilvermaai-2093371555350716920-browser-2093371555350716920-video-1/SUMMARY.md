# 编号时装章节｜同一造型跨天气、材质与姿势回归

## 用途

锁定成年模特的脸、发色和核心外套，用编号章节控制场景与材质变化；每章只测试一个视觉变量，结尾回到完整人物与总标题。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one clearly adult original fashion subject with at least three stable identity anchors. + Rotate distinct style modules while changing only declared wardrobe, accessory, palette and typography variables. + Bind every module to subject action, camera movement and foreground motion while preserving identity. + Insert one authored low-density pause after the maximum-intensity phase. + Return prior modules as a compressed recall and finish on the same adult in one stable hero frame.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 原创成年模特保持银色短发、钴蓝长外套和三角黄铜耳环，标题为【FIELD NOTES】。；章节【02 / SALT】与【03 / GLASS】只改变环境和反射光，外套不变。；回到开场黑底，同一脸、外套、耳环与准确标题【FIELD NOTES / 05】共同停留。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- At least three adult identity anchors persist.
- One exact numbered chapter controls each environment.
- Detail shots reconnect to the full-body identity.
- The final card restores all anchors and master title.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
