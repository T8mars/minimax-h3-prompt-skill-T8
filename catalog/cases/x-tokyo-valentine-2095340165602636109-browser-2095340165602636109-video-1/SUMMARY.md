# 人生字场旅程｜手写触发、空间短词与眼神收束

## 用途

先用成年人物的一次书写动作提出主题，再让原创短词依次变成圆环、道路、台阶和环境标记；人物持续穿过这些文字结构，终局回到同一身份的眼神或静止主标。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one clearly adult original subject, three identity anchors and one continuous world axis. + Bind each decisive action to exactly one short original semantic word that appears only after the action begins. + Make each word occupy, divide, reflect or redirect the physical environment rather than float as a subtitle. + Escalate typography scale and spatial depth while preserving identity, travel direction and readable action. + Return to the same adult face or full-body anchor and resolve the word sequence into one terminal identity proof.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 锁定原创成年城市规划师：黑色短发、锈红外套、黄铜笔记本，位于蓝调时刻的安静车站。；她穿过通道，准确短词【CROSS】变成通往远端站台的琥珀色地面横条。；以稳定眼神收束，全部结构折叠成可读原创句【MAKE THE NEXT MAP】并同时静止。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- One clearly adult identity and one initial writing action establish authorship of the word system.
- Each new exact short word follows one visible action and becomes one distinct environmental structure.
- Previous words remain traceable as later chapters increase scale or density.
- The final close view recombines all semantic stages into one exact original sentence and a stable personal reaction.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
