# 建筑字形穿行｜空间构件、尺度碰撞与广场回标

## 用途

先建立白色公共建筑和微小行人的稳定尺度，再让黑色字形构件与门洞、圆环和立柱共用空间骨架；镜头连续穿过可读孔洞，字形由标记升级为空间实体，终局回到完整广场。

## 推荐输入格式

`Declare a small exact text set, one restricted palette and one primary title before animation begins. + Build the primary title from visible components and reuse one of its letterform structures as the layout carrier. + Give each secondary word one distinct readable physical action inside the inherited structure. + Compress prior cards or grids into one central collision while preserving exact spelling. + Resolve all fragments into one complete terminal lockup and stop background, decoration and foreground together.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 建立原创象牙白雕塑园区，一名穿钴蓝外套的成年导览员横穿网格广场。；镜头跟随导览员穿过圆拱并从横梁下通过，同一批构件拆成三个可读空间分栏。；镜头拉远，把完整园区、导览员和重新组装的标题【AXIS / ARRIVE】统一在静止建筑主标中。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- One architectural site, one adult scale anchor and one movement axis establish the baseline.
- Every typographic component is built from visible structures already registered in that site.
- Letterforms may separate or enlarge but cannot replace the established architecture with an unrelated scene.
- The terminal wide shot reunites complete site, subject and exact original title in one stable lockup.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
